Let e=EL, U=integral_s^t E^4, and Z=integral_s^t D/E. All E,L,D,ell
are the actual weighted material integrals of the forward position family.
The new module invokes previously proved normal-flow first variations and
actual energy positivity. It does not introduce an abstract substitute for
the position, force, energy, or dissipation.

1. The compact-interval Cauchy--Schwarz lemma is proved from the nonnegative
integral of (f+ag)^2 for every real a, then the quadratic discriminant.
Continuity is needed only on Icc s t. The argument handles s=t and vanishing
quadratic coefficients; it does not divide by the integral of g^2.

2. NormalizedProduct supplies (e^2)'<=E^4 with its exact sum-of-squares
deficit. Applying its integral form from x to t and monotonicity of the
nonnegative E^4 integral gives e(x)^2>=e(t)^2-U throughout [s,t].
Nonnegative actual E,L permit the square-root conclusion. No record maximum
or monotonicity of e or L is assumed. The strict budget U<e(t)^2 ensures
the square-root argument and eventual denominator are positive.

3. The product FTC identity is e(t)-e(s)=integral E*ell-integral L*D.
Continuity holds on the closed nonnegative-time interval. Differentiability
is used only on its interior, where x>0 follows from s>=0. Thus s=0 is
included without backwards extension or a derivative hypothesis at zero.

4. Weighted Cauchy--Schwarz uses f=E^2 and g=ell/E, hence
  (integral E*ell)^2 <= U * integral(ell/E)^2 <= 2 U Z.
All divisions occur where actual E>0; the second inequality follows from
ell^2<=2ED. No unjustified assumption ell>=0 appears.

5. With m=sqrt(e(t)^2-U), the pointwise bound e>=m and D/E>=0 give
m Z<=integral L D. The endpoint-gain hypothesis e(s)<=e(t), together with
the exact product FTC identity, then gives m Z<=integral E ell. This also
proves that the latter integral is nonnegative before it is squared.
Combining the two inequalities gives m^2 Z^2<=2UZ. The code separates
Z=0 before canceling the positive Z in the other branch. The result is
  (e(t)^2-U) Z <= 2 U,
and division by the strictly positive coefficient gives the stated ratio.

Scope: this is a conditional, physical-time window theorem. Endpoint gain
and the budget comparison remain explicit inputs. It proves no energy-clock
inverse, first-record existence, uniform bound for EL, quarter-power bound,
ancient compactness, or contradiction from local straightening. The author's
reported actual check (672 declarations, standard three axioms, no warnings)
was not independently repeated by this review.
