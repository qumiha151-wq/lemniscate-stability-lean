Scalar energy argument
The helpers explicitly assume E>0, continuity on nonnegative time, E'=-D
on strictly positive time, and D>=c E^5. These are honestly scalar premises.
The actual inverse differentiation is 4D/E^5. Subtracting 4ct gives a
nondecreasing function, hence E(t)^(-4)>=E(0)^(-4)+4ct. Positivity justifies
each inversion and fourth-root comparison. fourthRoot is the actual
sqrt(sqrt(x)); for nonnegative x its fourth power is x. The positive-shift
envelope follows from E(0)^4*tau<=B^4 and 1<=4cB^4, with no missing sign.
Continuity plus the mean-value theorem includes t=0; no derivative at zero
or extension to backward normal FEF is used.

Weighted length argument
The elementary square identity gives ell<=aD+E/(2a) from ell^2<=2ED,
E,D>=0 and a>0, whether ell is positive or negative. Taking a=sqrt(t+tau),
the derivative of L+sqrt(t+tau)E is at most E/sqrt(t+tau). The energy
envelope bounds this by B/(t+tau)^(3/4), exactly the derivative of
4B*fourthRoot(t+tau). The proof uses the actual derivatives and a compact
interval mean-value argument, so no integrability beyond the stated
continuity is hidden. It does not assume monotone length or bounded EL.

The zero-shift theorem is a parameter limit delta down to zero, not an
improper time integral. Any positive envelope shift tau yields the weaker
envelope for 0<delta<=tau, because the positive denominator is smaller and
B>=0. Both sides are continuous at delta=0 and the right-neighborhood
filter is nontrivial. This retains the nonnegative term sqrt(t)E(t) in the
final bound. The coercive wrapper chooses tau=B^4/E(0)^4>0, so its required
positive-shift hypothesis is derived, not supplied as another assumption.

Actual curve adapter and constants
The extra hypothesis is explicitly the uniform bound on the genuine
material absolute curvature: for every t>=0, c.absoluteCurvature(t)<=A0.
It is not a bound on an unrelated scalar or on signed turning. The earlier
actual arclength adapter supplies A>=1 and E>0; therefore A0>0 follows.
Existing actual static coercivity gives
  D >= E^5/(262144 A(t)^6) >= E^5/(262144 A0^6).
All scalar continuity, normal velocity derivative and weighted ell^2<=2ED
premises are discharged by the frozen forward position-flow chain.

For c0=1/(262144 A0^6) and B=16 A0 sqrt(A0),
B^4=65536 A0^6 and 4c0B^4=1. The precise consequences are
  E(t) <= [E(0)^(-4)+t/(65536 A0^6)]^(-1/4),
  L(t)+sqrt(t)E(t) <= L(0)+64 A0 sqrt(A0)*fourthRoot(t),
and hence the displayed length-increment bound. The constants, signs and
geometric scaling agree with the scalar derivation. The proof does not
assume E tends to zero; the conditional decay estimate supplies that rate.

Boundary of the result
The global actual absolute-curvature cap is NOT proved. Neither do these
modules prove arbitrary-data global existence, unconditioned quarter-power
growth, finite zero count, a clock inverse, or an ancient compactness theorem.
They prove the stated conditional implication for an existing forward
classical normal FEF. The separate four-module integration and any remote
CI are later checks and are not claimed by these retained leaf builds.
