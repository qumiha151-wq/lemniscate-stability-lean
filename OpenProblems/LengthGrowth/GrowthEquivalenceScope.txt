ACTUAL FOUR-PROPERTY EQUIVALENCE AND EXACT RELATIVE ENERGY
13 November 2026

This four-module addition is a reduction for one given genuine forward
classical normal free elastic flow, with the existing actual initial-jet
regularity. It does not prove that any of its boundedness properties holds
for every flow and does not construct a new global solution.

AbsoluteCurvatureCS starts with actual arbitrary-speed smooth closed
immersions. The checked arclength transport and Cauchy--Schwarz prove
A^2<=2EL. Existing static coercivity then gives the deliberately weak
positive virial coefficient D>=E^2/(2097152 L^3), together with L>0.
No sharp 4*pi^2 theorem is claimed by this addition.

EnergyFromLength is a scalar lemma. Given positive L,E, continuous E at
nonnegative times, E'=-D at positive times, c,A>0, D>=cE^2/L^3, and
L<=A*fourthRoot(1+t), it derives
  E(t)^(-1)>=E(0)^(-1)+(4c/A^3)*(fourthRoot(1+t)-1),
  E(t)<=B/fourthRoot(1+t), and E(t)L(t)<=AB,
where B=E(0)+A^3/(4c). It needs no derivative, monotonicity, or continuity
of L. The actual wrapper supplies every scalar assumption geometrically.

GrowthEquivalence gives three iff statements connecting four properties:
  a uniform finite bound for actual total absolute curvature A(t);
  a uniform finite bound for the actual product E(t)L(t);
  a positive K with L(t)<=K*fourthRoot(1+t) for all t>=0;
  a positive B with E(t)<=B/fourthRoot(1+t) for all t>=0.
The actual normal FEF is an explicit premise of the dynamical equivalences.
Witness constants may depend on the entire individual flow. The code does
not assert a witness exists universally. Bounded A gives quarter length
by the earlier conditional theorem; quarter length gives decay and bounded
EL by EnergyFromLength with c=2^(-21); bounded EL gives bounded A by actual
Cauchy--Schwarz; quarter energy gives quarter length by weighted comparison.
No circular use of a desired cap is present. The older human proof in
quarter_growth_equivalence.txt is preserved as historical evidence; this
formal version uses the stated weaker coefficient and additionally includes
bounded absolute curvature as the fourth equivalent property.

RelativeEnergy independently proves exact physical-time accounting. With
U=int_s^t E^4 and Z=int_s^t D/E, it proves
  Z=log E(s)-log E(t), E(s)/E(t)=exp Z,
  U exp(-4Z)<=E(t)^4(t-s)<=U,
  (E(s)-E(t))/E(t)=exp Z-1, for 0<=s<=t.
It does not require endpoint product gain or an absolute-curvature cap.
It does not construct a clock inverse, first-record window, ancient limit
or contradiction from local straightening. Initial time and equal endpoints
are included; the PDE is used only at positive interior times.
