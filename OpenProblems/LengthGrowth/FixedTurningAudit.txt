INDEPENDENT AUDIT OF fixed_turning_growth_obstruction.txt
Reviewer: classification_new_approach subagent, 2026-11-12.
This is an analytic audit, not Lean verification or a priority determination.

I checked the elliptic-wave identities, the exact polynomial coefficients,
the explicit N>=12|q| threshold, and the q=0 closure-correction argument.
No substantive gap was found in those deductions.

1. For m=2/3, cn''=(2m-1)cn-2m cn^3 gives precisely
K''+K^3/2=(omega^2/3)K for K=2omega sqrt(m)cn(omega s).
The derivative of the arcsine expression is K because dn>0 and
sqrt(1-m sn^2)=dn. The claimed smooth periodic angle is therefore valid.
Half-period antisymmetry gives all the stated odd-moment cancellations.

2. The paired-integrand proof E_e/K_e>=sqrt(1-m) is correct:
sqrt(a)+sqrt(b)=sqrt(ab)(1/sqrt(a)+1/sqrt(b)), with
ab=1-m+m^2 sin^2 cos^2. Hence at m=2/3,
A2/(2L)-lambda >= (2/sqrt(3)-1)omega^2 > omega^2/7.
The last strict inequality reduces to 49>48. No decimal estimates enter.

3. Closure for q!=0 follows from the N-fold rotation geometric series.
The assumption N>=12|q| ensures R!=I and R^N=I, including negative q.
Since the tangent is smooth, periodic, and unit length, its zero integral
gives an actual smooth immersed closed curve with the asserted turning q.

4. Expanding F and squaring independently gives the D coefficients
lambda^2 A2, q^2(3lambda A2+9A4/4), (15/4)q^4 A2, Lq^6/4.
Multiplication of E and L' and subtraction of LD gives exactly the stated
U' coefficients; the constant term cancels and the N^2 coefficient is
-2q^4 L A2. Thus these are genuine geometric initial derivatives.
The upper bound A4<=(8/3)omega^2 A2 yields the coefficient -41/6.
The positivity polynomial has P(144)=10/7 and P'>0 on [144,infinity).
The numerical threshold is therefore rigorous.

5. For q=0 (indeed any q), the base tangent T0 is not confined to one
unoriented line: continuity and unit speed would then make it constant,
contradicting closure. The two independent normal-direction bump integrals
and hence invertible limiting Jacobian therefore exist.
The mean of exp(i phi) is r=2E_e/K_e-1>0: its imaginary part cancels by
half-period antisymmetry, and its real part is 1-2m sn^2.

The averaging estimates follow by integrating the bounded periodic
primitive of g-r against each smooth periodic test function; N integer
makes the endpoint terms cancel. The Hessian of C_N is uniformly bounded
because differentiating in alpha only inserts products of fixed hj and
the complex phase has modulus 1. Thus the fixed-radius derivative bound
and the shrinking ball of radius 2||J^-1 C_N(0)|| are legitimate. A zero
residual case merely gives the degenerate singleton ball and alpha_N=0.
The Banach construction gives exact closure and alpha_N=O(1/N), not just
approximate closure.

6. Writing b_N=theta0'+alpha_N.h', all its fixed-order derivatives are
uniformly bounded. Hence k_N=NK(Ns)+b_N and F_N=lambda N^3K(Ns)+O(N^2)
hold uniformly; the four displayed leading-order integral limits follow.
They have a strictly positive U'/N^6 limit, so the q=0 extension is sound.
The uniform position convergence gamma_N -> r beta follows by the same
bounded-primitive argument, now on variable intervals, plus alpha_N->0.

LIMIT: This constructs different initial curves with arbitrarily high EL
and positive initial (EL)'. It neither supplies one trajectory with EL
unbounded in time nor disproves the quarter-power long-time growth rate.
The note states this distinction correctly. Its D/E^5 limit additionally
rules out a turning-number-only positive coercivity constant of that form.
