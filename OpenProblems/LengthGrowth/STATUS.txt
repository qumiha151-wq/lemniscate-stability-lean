LENGTH GROWTH: PARTIAL FORMALIZATION AND A REVIEWED GEOMETRIC OBSTRUCTION
12 November 2026

ScalarDissipation.lean derives a secant estimate from genuine derivative
hypotheses and proves a conditional fourth-power length bound. The geometric
PDE identities and the assumed normalized-energy bound are not formalized
or proved here. This does not solve unrestricted quarter-power growth.

FixedTurningObstruction.txt constructs smooth closed immersed curves in every
fixed turning-number class with EL arbitrarily large and initially increasing.
For nonzero turning number the construction and threshold are explicit.
The same curves have D/E^5 tending to zero. An independent analytic review
found no substantive gap; its scope is recorded in FixedTurningAudit.txt.

These are initial data varying with an index, not one evolving solution with
unbounded EL. Thus this obstruction does not answer the long-time question.
It rules out universal high-EL monotonicity thresholds and a coercivity bound
depending only on turning number. Dependence on the full initial shape is
still possible. The construction is NOT Lean-formalized.

check_fixed_turning.py is an optional numerical check of the explicit
identities; it is not part of the Lean proof or a substitute for the argument.
