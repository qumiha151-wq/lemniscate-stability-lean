Independent analytic review by the root agent, 12 November 2026.

I checked the force expansion, integral orders, closure correction, and the
finite-energy obstruction in coercivity_sharpness.txt. No substantive gap
was found. This is an analytic review, not a Lean proof or novelty check.

At m=1/2 the cn identity gives K''+K^3/2=0 exactly, eliminating the N^3
force term. For fixed nonzero q, squaring the remaining three terms and
canceling odd moments gives the displayed D polynomial. The q^4 N^2
coefficient is 9/4+3/2=15/4; multiplication of E and L' and subtraction
of LD gives the negative U' polynomial. Cauchy--Schwarz makes its leading
coefficient at most -3 A2^2/4, hence strictly negative for q nonzero.

For general q the two-parameter exact closure construction is justified by
the positive mean r and two nonparallel tangent directions. Its correction
is O(1/N) with fixed smooth bump functions. Thus all fixed derivatives of
b_N are bounded and b_N converges smoothly to the base curvature. The
displayed exact force contains b_N'' and has leading term
(3/2)b_N N^2 K(Ns)^2. Squaring and periodic averaging proves the positive
D_N/N^4 limit, including turning number zero since every smooth closed
unit-speed base curve has strictly positive energy.

The N^3 K^3 term in L' does not spoil the limit: its coefficient is smooth
periodic with uniformly bounded second derivative, and two integrations by
parts against the bounded periodic primitives give an O(N^-2) integral.
This proves the stated negative leading U'/N^4 limit.

Since E_N is asymptotic to a positive multiple of N^2 and L is fixed, the
failure of every homogeneous coercivity exponent alpha>2 follows. The
statement concerns the exponent; it does not establish the optimal constant.

For disjoint intervals, the angle-change inequality followed by two
Cauchy--Schwarz estimates gives 2E >= A^2*n^2/L. This correctly rules out
direct infinite packing of the fixed-angle cells in finite initial energy.
It does not rule out the subsequent creation of oscillations by the flow.

The note consistently distinguishes families of different initial curves
from one long-time trajectory. It does not settle unrestricted length
growth, general migration, or all self-similar profile classification.
