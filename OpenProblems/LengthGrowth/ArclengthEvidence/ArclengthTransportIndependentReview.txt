1. The auxiliary premise is the actual HasDerivAt eta with derivative
   1/speed(gamma)(eta s). It is explicitly present, and no inverse identity or
   global existence is smuggled into these auxiliary lemmas. My separately
   constructed ArclengthSmooth.inverse_hasDerivAt supplies exactly this
   premise for the actual global inverse in the final adapter. The source
   comment correctly distinguishes these two stages.

2. The scalar chain rule produces actual derivative of f o eta equal to the
   existing arcDerivative gamma f evaluated at eta. It works for the real
   normed-space generality stated. Position differentiation is the same
   chain rule; the tangent norm theorem then proves unit speed and regularity.
   Positive reciprocal speed fixes the signed orientation.

3. Tangent and normal transport are actual definition equalities after the
   proved speed identity. Curvature transport uses the actual normal inner
   product of DsT. Smoothness and regularity of gamma o eta are supplied by
   proved chain consequences, not independently assumed geometric fields.
   Rewriting the entire tangent function before differentiation is essential
   and is present. The curvatureGradient and curvatureSecond proofs likewise
   rewrite whole functions before taking the next actual derivative. Thus
   the force identity includes both intrinsic derivatives and the cubic term.

4. The legacy bridge checks the definitions, rather than identifying names:
   under actual unit speed, tangent beta = deriv beta, speed beta = 1, and
   Im(conj(beta') beta'') equals ActualFlow.curvature. Whole-function equality
   is used before two ordinary derivatives in the legacy force. These last
   algebraic identities do not require extra smoothness assumptions because
   Lean's total deriv respects function equality; the eventual actual curve
   is smooth by construction.

5. Imports are only LengthGrowth/Mathlib transitively. No CentroidEscape
   theorem, abstract frame, force invariance, integral substitution, or
   unit-speed hypothesis on the original gamma is used. This module alone
   does not yet prove the energy/dissipation change of variables or the final
   arbitrary-speed static coercivity theorem, and does not make a time-gauge
   or global PDE existence assertion.
