SUB-SQUARE-ROOT LENGTH: SCALAR DISSIPATION CONSEQUENCE
13 November 2026. Independent LengthGrowth project.

Theorem FreeElasticScalar.restart_length_div_sqrt_tendsto_zero proves
L(t)/sqrt(t) -> 0 as t -> infinity, from only:
  H(s)>=0 for s>=0;
  (L(t)-L(s))^2 <= (t-s)*(H(s)-H(t)) for 0<=s<=t.
L need not be nonnegative. No continuity or differentiability is added to
these hypotheses. No assumption H(t)->0, or E(t)->0, is used.

Proof: take a=inf{H(s):s>=0}; choose S>=0 with H(S)<a+delta.
Since H(t)>=a for all t>=S, the restart estimate yields
L(t)^2 <= 2*t*delta+2*L(S)^2. Arbitrary delta>0 proves L(t)^2/t->0,
and continuity of square root proves the signed quotient conclusion.
The nonempty lower-bounded infimum, eventual thresholds, and all limit
steps are kernel-checked. An independent analytic review confirmed this
argument, including signed L and the absence of an energy-zero premise.

The earlier ScalarDissipation module derives the finite-interval restart
estimate from scalar derivatives and (L')^2<=-H'. For the geometric
application H=E^2. Its derivation from actual free-elastic-flow PDE data
remains outside this module, and the result is therefore recorded as a
scalar consequence, not as a completed formalization of the PDE theorem.
This improves the square-root scalar upper bound only. It neither proves
unrestricted O(t^(1/4)) growth nor supplies a counterexample to that rate.
