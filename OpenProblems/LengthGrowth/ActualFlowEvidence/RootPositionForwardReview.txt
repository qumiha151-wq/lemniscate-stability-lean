ROOT INDEPENDENT REVIEW: POSITION GEOMETRY AND THE FORWARD ENDPOINT
2026-11-13. Read-only mathematical/statement review of agent-authored sources.

The root read the full sources IntrinsicCurve, SpatialPeriodicity,
FamilyRegularity, FamilyDerivatives, FamilyPeriodicity, JetGeometry,
FourJetForce, ForwardGeometry and ForwardDissipation. This note reviews
those modules, not the root's own integration/first-variation proofs.
The latter have a separate independent review by finish_odd_rigidity.
All source/output identifiers are retained in the corresponding frozen
manifests and actual compile reports; no duplicate leaf compilation was
performed as part of this source review.

IntrinsicCurve defines g=|gamma_u|, T=g^-1 gamma_u, N=iT and the signed
curvature from actual derivatives. It proves unit/orthogonal frame,
gamma_u=gT, T_u=g*kappa*N and N_u=-g*kappa*T. The stress is
W=(kappa^2/2)T+kappa_s N and the actual identity W_u=g*F*N is proved by
product rules. No supplied Frenet field, curvature derivative or stress
identity replaces an actual position derivative. SpatialPeriodicity
derives all required periodicity from the actual position's periodicity.

FamilyRegularity contains only position, open-time joint smoothness,
fixed positive period, periodic position and immersion. V, a=<DsV,T>
and b=<DsV,N> are actual definitions. In FamilyDerivatives the actual
curried mixed derivatives are related to the root's proved Frechet
partial symmetry using equality on time neighbourhoods. It is applied
to both the position and tangent, using their proved joint smoothness.
The resulting identities are g_t=g*a, T_t=b*N, N_t=-b*T and
g*kappa_t+kappa*g_t=b_u. The final curvature identity follows by
cancelling the nonzero unit normal, not by assuming the curvature PDE.
FamilyPeriodicity obtains V-periodicity from equality of the position
at spatial translates on an open time neighbourhood. All three relevant
variation boundary terms then have equal endpoint values.

JetGeometry uses genuine successive spatial derivatives of gamma.
Its curvature formula is kappa=det(gamma_u,gamma_uu)/g^3. FourJetForce
differentiates that formula twice in arclength and proves the given
four-jet polynomial divided by g^9 equals the previously defined F.
The coefficients 7/2, 3/2 and 9/2, powers of g and n^3/2 match the
independent analytic calculation in ActualFEFFirstVariationLeanSpec.
The pure fourJetForceValue does not redefine the force; equality with
the intrinsic force is a proved theorem. Its filter-limit theorem needs
only convergence of the four actual jets and nonzero limiting first jet.

ForwardGeometry assumes smooth initial position, smooth interior family,
immersion and periodicity for t>=0, and joint continuity to t=0 of actual
iterated spatial derivatives of orders 0..4. These are explicit classical
geometric regularity assumptions, not an energy law or an independent
continuous force field. There is no time derivative required at zero,
and no FEF equation or geometric smoothness required at negative times.
The fact that Lean functions have domain R does not impose constraints
on their arbitrary negative-time values.

The actual two/four-jet formulas give continuity of g, kappa and F on
the closed forward domain. Fixed-parameter compact integration gives
continuity of L,E,D and ell there, including zero. The initial energy
and force are calculated from the actual initial immersion.

ForwardDissipation imposes V=-F*N only at strictly positive times.
It uses the already proved open-time variation formulas, then the FTC
version whose derivative hypothesis is only on Ioo(s,t). Therefore,
for 0<=s<=t, it proves the genuine identities
  E(t)+integral_s^t D=E(s),
  L(t)-L(s)=integral_s^t ell,
as well as (L(t)-L(s))^2 <= (t-s)*(E(s)^2-E(t)^2).
No backward extension, initial two-sided derivative, or limiting value
E(infinity)=0 is assumed. The existing scalar restart theorem applies
to actual H=E^2>=0 and yields L(t)/sqrt(t)->0 for the given global
forward classical normal FEF. Global existence is a hypothesis embodied
by such a family on the whole half-line, not a theorem proved here.

No mathematical or statement-scope gap was found. Imports remain in
Mathlib and LengthGrowth. These results complete the actual PDE-to-scalar
dissipation bridge and the one-sided endpoint treatment under the stated
regularity assumptions. They do not prove the unrestricted fourth-root
length estimate, unrestricted whole-curve migration, complete soliton
classification, or the existence of every initial-value flow.
