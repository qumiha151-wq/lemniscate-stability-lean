No substantive semantic gap found in this endpoint assembly.

1. The ForwardImmersion record contains an actual position, fixed positive
period, periodicity and immersion at nonnegative times, initial spatial
smoothness, interior joint smoothness, and continuity of the actual iterated
spatial derivatives through order four on the forward time domain. It does
not contain an independently assigned curvature, force, energy or variation
identity. Although position is a total function of real time, no property of
negative-time values is used to assert a backward solution.

2. The conversion from iteratedDeriv to positionJet1--4 uses actual recursive
derivative identities. The two-jet curvature formula and four-jet force
formula identify the existing intrinsic fields with their pure expressions.
The immersion hypothesis supplies a nonzero limiting first jet at t=0, so
the arbitrary-filter continuity argument applies to the forward topology.
This reviewer authored the jet modules; this is an independent review of the
two new endpoint modules, not an independent re-review of those jet proofs.

3. Compact spatial integration is justified on each finite closed time slab.
The local slab [0,t+1] is a neighbourhood within nonnegative times at t, which
correctly yields continuity up to zero of the actual L,E,D,ell integrals.
No global uniform lower speed bound is assumed or required by this argument.

4. The normal velocity condition is imposed only at t>0 and agrees exactly
with the interior SmoothPeriodicImmersion PDE. The derivatives of L and E
are inherited from the already proved actual first variation. The interval
FTC consumes endpoint continuity and derivatives only on the open interval
(s,t). For 0<=s<t, these derivative times are strictly positive. The s=t
restart branch is handled directly; no initial-time derivative is asserted.

5. The energy identity has E(t)+integral_s^t D=E(s), with the correct negative
sign in E'=-D. The length identity and squared secant inequality use actual
ell and the actual weighted Cauchy--Schwarz bound. Endpoint initial energy
is the same position-defined integral, not a free scalar constant.

6. The final L(t)/sqrt(t)->0 theorem invokes the existing scalar restart
theorem with H=E^2>=0 and the proved actual secant inequality for all forward
intervals. It assumes a global forward classical immersion with the stated
regularity and normal PDE. It neither constructs that flow for arbitrary
initial data nor assumes limiting energy is zero or length is monotone.
It does not give quarter-power growth or resolve the remaining general
length-growth conjecture.
