Convex and signed three-moment estimates for planar free elastic flow

Proof locations are relative to the independent LengthGrowth Lean project.
The first six files use Lean 4.33.1 and the project's pinned mathlib. The upper
bound for length is for the entire forward trajectory and allows an
initial-data-dependent prefactor. The corresponding energy estimate is
E(t) <= B/(1+t)^(1/4). Hypotheses imposed only for t>T allow an arbitrary
smooth prefix [0,T].

LengthGrowth/ActualFlow/EventualCurvatureSign.lean
  signedCurvature_hasDerivAt_zero and signedCurvature_eq_initial derive
  conservation of the actual signed curvature integral from the smooth
  immersion. The argument works for a regular homotopy. The final theorem
  quarter_bounds_of_eventual_spatial_curvature_sign applies the proved
  length-growth equivalence to an actual normal free elastic flow whose
  signed curvature is spatially nonnegative or nonpositive at each
  sufficiently late time. It uses compactness on the finite prefix and
  takes neither a turning number nor an absolute-curvature bound as input.

LengthGrowth/ActualFlow/SignedAreaVariation.lean
  materialSignedArea is the signed Green integral of x dy in the actual
  material parameter. integral_force_eq_half_cubic derives the spatial
  periodic cancellation; signedArea_hasDerivAt_normal proves A'=C/2
  for the actual position equation, where C is the integral of k^3.
  It covers arbitrary smooth immersed slices.

LengthGrowth/ActualFlow/ConvexAreaEnergy.lean
  curvature_cubic_moment proves (int k^2)^2 <= (int k)(int k^3) for
  nonnegative curvature. cubic_sq_le_four_length_dissipation proves
  C^2 <= 4 L D for either curvature sign. All integrals use the material
  speed. areaEnergy_hasDerivAt derives the exact product derivative.
  areaEnergy_antitoneOn_of_gage obtains monotonicity under explicit
  hypotheses of nonnegative curvature, initial signed total curvature
  2 pi, and the geometric Gage inequality pi L <= 2 A E at late slices.
  The geometric derivations of Gage's inequality, of the total turning
  2 pi of a positively oriented convex boundary and of the identification
  of the signed Green area with enclosed area are external inputs to
  the application in the manuscript.

LengthGrowth/ActualFlow/CubicMomentGrowth.lean
  CubicMomentDominates means (2 E)^3 <= L C^2. OneSidedHalfRMS uses the
  actual R=sqrt(2 E/L) and permits k >= -R/2 everywhere or k <= R/2
  everywhere on a slice. cubicMomentDominates_of_oneSidedHalfRMS derives
  the moment inequality by integrating a nonnegative polynomial.
  normalizedProduct_deriv_nonpos_of_cubicMoment and
  normalizedProduct_antitoneOn_of_eventual_cubicMoment give the derivative
  test and tail monotonicity of E L. quarter_bounds_of_eventual_cubicMoment
  and quarter_bounds_of_eventual_oneSidedHalfRMS give the length and energy
  estimates. These results use the universal Cauchy--Schwarz bound C^2<=4LD.
  No Gage inequality, turning-number assumption or convexity premise
  enters any of their proofs.

LengthGrowth/ActualFlow/CubicMomentDissipation.lean
  curvatureOscillation_eq_integral identifies K = L int (k-kbar)^2 ds
  with 2 E L minus squared conserved total signed curvature. The
  curvatureOscillation_exact_squares identity rewrites the derivative
  deficit as the sum of an actual squared force residual, a scalar square
  and a multiple of the cubic-moment excess. On slices satisfying the
  signed moment condition it gives 8 L^2 K' <= -(2 E)^2 K.

LengthGrowth/ActualFlow/CubicMomentDecay.lean
  curvatureOscillation_le_exp_integral integrates that differential
  inequality with the exact factor exp[-int (2E)^2/(8L^2) dt].
  curvatureOscillation_le_exp_rescaled_time gives exp[-int L^(-4) dt/8].
  oscillationDecayRate_integral_lower compares the exponent to a positive
  multiple of log(1+t). The final theorem
  curvatureOscillation_tendsto_zero_of_eventual_cubicMoment proves
  K(t) -> 0 from the eventual signed moment condition. Its length bound
  is derived in CubicMomentGrowth.lean. Standard library limits and
  the integrating factor give convergence in actual physical time.
  signedCurvature_sq_ge_one_of_eventual_cubicMoment excludes zero signed
  total curvature from this class using the project's elementary lower
  bound on total absolute curvature.

The eventual-convexity circular convergence and the limiting coefficients
in convex_free_elastic_flow.tex are analytic results. Its compactness
and restart argument uses the published stability theorem of Miura and
Wheeler. For the more general signed-moment class, the formal limit
K(t) -> 0 supplies the small-curvature-oscillation hypothesis of
Andrews and Wheeler (arXiv:2509.11129, Theorem 1). Smooth shape
convergence still uses that external stability theorem.

LengthGrowth/ActualFlow/CubicHalfThreshold.lean
  centered_cubic_deficit and normalized_cubic_deficit_bound prove the
  force-variance inequality in material and length-one variables.
  quarter_bounds_of_liminf_cubic_gt_half obtains both quarter-power
  estimates when the extended-real liminf of the absolute signed cubic
  ratio exceeds one half. exists_late_high_record_below_cubic_half_add
  gives the restriction on arbitrarily late records when quarter-power
  growth fails. See HalfThreshold.md for the exact barrier. The bounds
  at and below the half threshold and the circularity gap in the research
  manuscript are analytic results.

the axiom audit from the combined build.
