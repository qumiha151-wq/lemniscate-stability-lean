No semantic gap found. This is a source review; I did not rerun Lean.
The previously reported successful build is the author's/root's evidence.

1. SupportProfile assumes the actual derivatives U'=v, p'=-Uq,
   q'=1+Up on all of R. It assumes neither the elastic support equation
   for v' nor a sign/alignment condition at any zero of U.

2. radiusSq=p^2+q^2 has derivative 2q. Consequently
      balance=U(p^2+q^2)+2p,   balance'=v(p^2+q^2).
   The product cancellations in the source are exactly these identities.

3. If v is everywhere nonnegative, balance is monotone. Coordinate
   limits (-1,1,0) at -infinity and (1,-1,0) at +infinity give balance
   limits +1 and -1. The source obtains 1<=balance(0)<=-1 directly from
   monotonicity and the two limits. It never integrates on an infinite
   interval, so no decay, tail-integrability, finite-energy, or bounded
   variation hypothesis has been silently introduced.

4. The conclusion exists s, v(s)<0 is exactly a point of strictly
   decreasing actual curvature. The final not_monotone_curvature theorem
   uses the nonnegative derivative of a monotone differentiable function
   and identifies that derivative with v using U_deriv. Continuity of v
   is not needed for the stated result.

5. This is a necessary condition for a hypothetical connection. It does
   not establish a connection, determine the angular-velocity sign, or
   imply a length-growth estimate. Its namespace/imports keep the Length
   problem independent of other open-problem projects.
