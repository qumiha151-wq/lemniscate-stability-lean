WHAT THE INPUT MEANS

ClosedFrenetCurve contains an actual periodic position gamma : Real ->
Complex with positive period, its actual unit tangent gamma'=T,
T'=k*(I*T), actual real derivatives k'=k1 and k1'=k2, continuity of k2,
and the periodicity of T,k,k1. Normal is DEFINED to be I*T. The force
F is DEFINED as k2+k^3/2. Energy, dissipation, and absolute curvature are
their actual interval integrals. The closing integral integral T=0 is
proved from periodic gamma by FTC.

The final theorems have NO assumed stress bound, no assumed direction
cone, no assumed nonzero force, no assumed energy/coercivity inequality,
and no assumed numerical constants about a trajectory. The maximum,
stress nonvanishing under the contradiction hypothesis, all integral
estimates, and the final closing contradiction are proved internally.
