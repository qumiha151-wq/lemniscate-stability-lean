SHARPNESS OF STATIC DISSIPATION COERCIVITY AND THE MULTISCALE OBSTRUCTION
12 November 2026. Human proof, independently reviewed in the accompanying
CoercivitySharpnessAudit.txt; not Lean-formalized.
This note does NOT solve the long-time length-growth or migration questions.

THEOREM A (optimal energy exponent even at fixed turning number).
For every integer q and every real alpha>2, there are smooth closed unit-speed
immersions gamma_N of length L=2pi and turning number q such that

  E_N -> infinity,  D_N = O(E_N^2),
  D_N L^(5-alpha)/E_N^alpha -> 0,

where E=(1/2) integral k^2 and D=integral (k_ss+k^3/2)^2. In fact D_N/E_N^2
converges to a finite positive number. The curves can be kept in a common
bounded region and converge uniformly to a fixed nonconstant closed immersion.

Consequently no c(q)>0 yields D>=c(q) E^alpha/L^(5-alpha) for every closed
immersion of turning number q when alpha>2. The known virial estimate
D>=4pi^2 E^2/L^3 therefore has an optimal POWER of E among such homogeneous
bounds, even after fixing the turning number. This does not claim that its
numerical constant is optimal.

1. A PERIODIC FREE-EULER WAVE.

Take elliptic parameter m=1/2. Let K_e and E_e be its complete elliptic
integrals and let omega=2K_e/pi. Define, with parameter notation sn(u|m),

  phi(s)=2 arcsin(sqrt(m) sn(omega s|m)),
  K(s)=phi'(s)=2 omega sqrt(m) cn(omega s|m).

These are smooth 2pi-periodic real functions. The derivatives sn'=cn dn,
cn'=-sn dn, dn'=-m sn cn and positivity of dn give these identities directly.
Moreover

  K''+K^3/2=0,   K(s+pi)=-K(s),   max K^2=2 omega^2.

Write A2=integral_0^L K^2 and A4=integral_0^L K^4. Both are positive and
A2^2<=L A4 by Cauchy--Schwarz. All odd moments of K vanish.
The first integral K'^2+K^4/4=omega^4 and the integrated equation give
A4=(4/3)L omega^4, though this explicit value is not needed below.

The complex periodic tangent multiplier g=exp(i phi) has mean

  r=(1/L)integral g = 2 E_e/K_e-1 > 0.

To see positivity without decimal numerics, pair a=1-m sin^2 u and
b=1-m cos^2 u on [0,pi/2]. Since ab>=1-m,
sqrt(a)+sqrt(b)>=sqrt(1-m)(1/sqrt(a)+1/sqrt(b)). Integration yields
E_e/K_e>=sqrt(1-m)=1/sqrt(2), so r>=sqrt(2)-1>0.

References for the elementary special-function identities and periods:
NIST DLMF https://dlmf.nist.gov/22.13 and https://dlmf.nist.gov/22.4,
checked 12 November 2026. DLMF uses modulus k, whereas this note uses m=k^2.
The subsequent closed-curve construction and estimates are deductions here;
no novelty or publication claim is made.

2. EXPLICIT CLOSURE FOR q!=0.

For an integer N>|q| let theta_N(s)=qs+phi(Ns), T_N=exp(i theta_N), and
 gamma_N(s)=integral_0^s T_N(u) du.
As in the previous m=2/3 construction, successive cells of length L/N are
rotated by exp(2pi i q/N). Their geometric series sums to zero, so gamma_N
is EXACTLY closed and has unit speed and turning number q.
Its curvature is k_N=q+N K(Ns). Because K''+K^3/2=0, the leading N^3 term
in its elastic force cancels exactly:

  F_N=(3q/2)N^2 K(Ns)^2+(3q^2/2)N K(Ns)+q^3/2.

The actual geometric quantities are therefore

  E_N=(Lq^2+N^2 A2)/2,
  D_N=(9/4)q^2 A4 N^4+(15/4)q^4 A2 N^2+Lq^6/4,
  L'_N=3q^2 A2 N^2+Lq^4/2.

In particular

  D_N/E_N^2 -> 9q^2 A4/A2^2 >0.

These are actual closed curves, not scalar histories satisfying selected
inequalities. The exact force expansion proves the asymptotic exponents.

For clarity, this family is NOT the earlier positive-U' family. Here

  (EL)'_N=q^2[(3/2)A2^2-(9/4)L A4]N^4-2q^4 L A2 N^2 <0,

because A2^2<=L A4. Thus being nearly extremal for the POWER in the virial
estimate does not by itself cause normalized energy to increase. This is a
useful obstruction to interpreting a small D/E^5 ratio dynamically.

3. ALL TURNING NUMBERS, INCLUDING ZERO.

Choose ANY smooth closed unit-speed immersion beta of length L=2pi and
turning number q; write T0=exp(i theta0) and b0=theta0'. Its energy is
positive because a closed unit-speed curve cannot have constant tangent.
Choose smooth periodic real h1,h2 so that the two real vectors
 integral i T0 h1, integral i T0 h2
are independent. This is possible by bump localization near two nonparallel
tangent directions. A closed smooth unit-speed immersion necessarily has
such directions.

Set
 C_N(a)=integral_0^L T0(s) g(Ns) exp(i(a1 h1(s)+a2 h2(s))) ds.
The mean-zero periodic function g-r has a bounded smooth periodic primitive.
Integration by parts against smooth periodic test functions gives
 C_N(0)=O(1/N),  DC_N(0)=J+O(1/N),
where J has columns r integral iT0 hj and is invertible. Hessians of C_N
are uniformly bounded because all complex phases have modulus one and the
hj are fixed. On a fixed small ball, a -> a-J^-1 C_N(a) is a contraction
with constant at most 1/2 for large N; it preserves the smaller ball of radius
2||J^-1 C_N(0)||. Hence an EXACT zero a_N exists with a_N=O(1/N).

Let
 theta_N=theta0+phi(Ns)+a_N.h,
 gamma_N(s)=integral_0^s exp(i theta_N(u)) du,
 b_N=theta0'+a_N.h'.
The resulting curve is closed, smooth, unit-speed and of turning q. Every
fixed derivative of b_N is uniformly bounded, and b_N converges smoothly to
b0. Its curvature and force are EXACTLY

 k_N=NK(Ns)+b_N,
 F_N=(3/2)b_N N^2 K(Ns)^2+(3/2)b_N^2 N K(Ns)+b_N^3/2+b_N''.

Periodic averaging now gives

 E_N/N^2 -> A2/2,
 D_N/N^4 -> (9A4/(4L)) integral_0^L b0^2 >0.

For example, the latter follows by dividing the displayed F_N by N^2:
the remainder is uniformly O(1/N), and K^4(Ns) has mean A4/L. Thus

 D_N/E_N^2 -> [9A4/(L A2^2)] integral b0^2 >0.

Since L is fixed, the asserted failure of every alpha>2 follows. Uniform
position convergence gamma_N -> r beta (with matching initial point) follows
by the bounded-primitive integration estimate and a_N->0; the curves therefore
remain in one bounded region. This does not assert embeddedness.

One can also verify that these general-q curves have initially decreasing EL.
Expansion gives
 L'_N=integral[(3/2)b_N N^3 K^3+3b_N^2 N^2 K^2
                 +N K(2b_N^3+b_N'')+b_N^4/2+b_N b_N''].
The N^3 term divided by N^2 tends to zero: K^3 is mean zero, and integrating
by parts twice gives integral b_N K^3(Ns)=O(N^-2), uniformly in N. Thus
 L'_N/N^2 -> (3A2/L) integral b0^2,
 (EL)'_N/N^4 -> [(3A2^2/(2L))-(9A4/4)] integral b0^2 <0.
(The coefficient 2b_N^3 in the N term is harmless for this limit.)

4. WHY DIRECT MULTISCALE PATCHING DOES NOT PRODUCE ONE COUNTEREXAMPLE.

Let a finite-energy unit-speed curve have length L and E=(1/2)integral k^2.
If disjoint parameter intervals I_1,...,I_n each have tangent-angle variation
at least A between two selected points, then

 A <= integral_{I_j}|k| <= sqrt(|I_j| integral_{I_j} k^2).

Consequently
 2E >= A^2 sum_j 1/|I_j| >= A^2 n^2/L,
so n <= sqrt(2EL)/A. The same estimate also shows that a selected angle change
A requires interval length at least A^2/(2E).

The positive-U' elliptic-wave construction uses m>1/2 and therefore has an
order-one tangent excursion on every cell (2 arcsin(sqrt(m))>pi/2). Infinitely
many such cells cannot simply be patched into a single finite-length,
finite-energy initial immersion. Shrinking their spatial sizes only increases
the energy cost. Smoothness supplies an even stronger local obstruction via
a bounded curvature and uniform continuity of the tangent.

This excludes a direct diagonal argument from the sequence gamma_N to one
smooth initial datum carrying all its cells. It does NOT exclude a dynamical
creation of oscillations as length grows, nor a different small-amplitude
construction, nor concentration in normalized coordinates.

5. PRECISE LIMITS AND NEXT MATHEMATICAL QUESTION.

All constructions here vary the initial curve. They do not yield a single
trajectory whose EL becomes unbounded; the time derivative for Theorem A is
actually negative. The earlier m=2/3 examples have positive initial (EL)', but
still do not supply unbounded growth along one trajectory.

The exact equivalence in work/global_length/quarter_growth_equivalence.txt
remains the correct target: a long-time counterexample must have EL unbounded.
This note proves that improving the static virial estimate to any higher
energy power using only turning number is impossible, even on C0-bounded
families. Any proof of universal quarter-power growth must therefore use
additional dynamical information or constants depending on more initial data.
No full classification or migration result is inferred.
