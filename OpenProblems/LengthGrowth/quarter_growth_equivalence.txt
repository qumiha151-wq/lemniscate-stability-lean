EXACT REDUCTION OF THE UNRESTRICTED LENGTH-GROWTH QUESTION
Human proof; not yet Lean-formalized. 11 November 2026.

Let gamma(t) be any smooth closed planar free elastic flow, and use
E=(1/2)integral k^2 ds, L=length, D=integral F^2 ds. Assume only the
already established geometric identities/estimates:

  E'=-D,
  |L(t)-L(s)|^2 <= (t-s)(E(s)^2-E(t)^2) for 0<=s<=t,
  D >= 4pi^2 E^2/L^3,
  EL>=2pi^2.

The following three properties of this individual solution are equivalent:

  (A) sup_{t>=0} E(t)L(t) < infinity;
  (B) there exists A<infinity with L(t)<=A(1+t)^(1/4) for all t>=0;
  (C) there exists B<infinity with E(t)<=B(1+t)^(-1/4) for all t>=0.

In particular, the original existence question for growth not O(t^(1/4))
is exactly equivalent to the existence of a trajectory with unbounded EL.
Uniform boundedness of EL is not merely a stronger sufficient hypothesis.

Proof (A) implies (B).

This is the existing doubling argument. If EL<=K and L(t)>2L(0), choose
s with L(s)=L(t)/2. Then L(t)^2/4<=tE(s)^2<=4K^2 t/L(t)^2, hence
L(t)^4<=16K^2t. Consequently

  L(t)<=max(2L(0),2sqrt(K)t^(1/4))
       <=max(2L(0),2sqrt(K))(1+t)^(1/4).

Proof (B) implies (C).

Positivity of E follows from Fenchel. Differentiate 1/E and use the
virial inequality and (B):

  (1/E)'=D/E^2 >=4pi^2/L^3
                >=(4pi^2/A^3)(1+t)^(-3/4).

Integration gives, with d=16pi^2/A^3 and w=(1+t)^(1/4)>=1,

  1/E(t)>=1/E(0)+d(w-1)>=min(1/E(0),d)*w.

Thus (C) holds with B=max(E(0),A^3/(16pi^2)).

Proof (C) implies (B).

This proof uses no differential upper bound for L and no monotonicity of L.
For t>=1 choose n>=0 so that r=t/2^n lies in [1,2). For j=1,...,n the
restart estimate, E>=0, and (C) give

  |L(2^j r)-L(2^(j-1)r)|
    <= sqrt(2^(j-1)r) E(2^(j-1)r)
    <= B (2^(j-1)r)^(1/4).

Summing the geometric series,

  L(t)<=L(r)+[B/(2^(1/4)-1)]t^(1/4)
       <=L(0)+sqrt(2)E(0)+[B/(2^(1/4)-1)]t^(1/4).

The last inequality uses the original restart estimate on [0,r]. On
[0,1], L(t)<=L(0)+E(0). Therefore (B) holds globally with, for example,

  A=L(0)+sqrt(2)E(0)+B/(2^(1/4)-1).

Finally (B) and (C) imply (A) immediately by multiplying. Since (C)
implies (B), this closes the equivalence chain.

Two-sided rates.

Under these equivalent conditions, Fenchel EL>=2pi^2 combines with (B)
to give E(t)>=(2pi^2/A)(1+t)^(-1/4), and combines with (C) to give
L(t)>=(2pi^2/B)(1+t)^(1/4). Hence both E and L have two-sided quarter
power rates. In particular there is no third case where length has the
quarter-power upper bound but normalized energy still diverges.

Limitations.

This proves an equivalence, not that any one of (A)--(C) holds for every
trajectory. The smooth scalar countermodel in scalar_obstruction.txt
satisfies all four starting conditions and violates all three properties.
Therefore a proof of the unrestricted theorem still needs an additional
geometric estimate or an actual geometric counterexample.
