Definitions and quantifiers
--------------------------
All four properties concern one fixed genuine ForwardImmersion c and all
physical times t>=0. Absolute curvature, energy and length retain their
actual geometric definitions; none is a new abstract scalar placeholder.
HasBoundedAbsoluteCurvature and HasBoundedEnergyLength existentially quantify
a real cap; positivity is then recovered from genuine closed-curve facts.
HasQuarterLengthBound and HasQuarterEnergyBound explicitly require a
positive witness. Their weights are exactly q(1+t)=sqrt(sqrt(1+t)).
No witness is uniform over distinct initial curves or distinct flows.

Five directions
---------------
1. Bounded EL => bounded absolute curvature is purely static. At t=0,
nonnegative E,L imply K>=0. Actual Cauchy--Schwarz gives A(t)^2<=2E(t)L(t)
<=2K, so sqrt(2K) is a valid cap. The use of actual A(t)>=1 is legitimate;
there is no hidden assumed total-curvature bound. In AbsoluteCurvatureCS,
the arbitrary-speed integral transport is supplied by the already checked
arclength construction. Cubing A^2<=2EL and combining the existing
E^5/(262144*A^6) dissipation lower bound gives exactly
  D >= E^2/(2097152*L^3).
All canceled energies and lengths are strictly positive by actual geometry.
The number is deliberately 2^(-21), not the unformalized sharp 4*pi^2.

2. Bounded absolute curvature => quarter length invokes the already proved
actual conditional increment. A>0 follows from A(0)>=1. With
C=64*A*sqrt(A)>0, the increment L(t)<=L(0)+C*q(t), the monotonicity
q(t)<=q(1+t), and q(1+t)>=1 give witness K=L(0)+C>0. No normal-flow
assumption is silently dropped: this direction explicitly receives hflow.

3. Quarter length => quarter energy supplies all scalar assumptions to
EnergyFromLength from actual geometry: positive length/energy, energy
continuity, actual E'=-D only at positive time, and the just-proved weak
virial. The explicit scalar length envelope is exactly the existential
witness extracted from the incoming property. Its positive energy witness
is B=E(0)+K^3/(4a), a=1/2097152. No stronger virial or abstract energy law
is assumed in the final theorem.

4. Quarter length => bounded EL invokes the companion scalar result with
the same actual quantities and hypotheses. The resulting witness is K*B.
The product cap is derived; it is not used to justify the energy estimate.

5. Quarter energy => quarter length invokes weighted_length_zero_shift
with tau=1, the positive incoming B, actual continuity of E and L, actual
normal first variations and actual ell^2<=2ED. The envelope argument is
only the commutation 1+s=s+1. The previously proved positive-shift limit
therefore gives L(t)+sqrt(t)E(t)<=L(0)+4B*q(t). Nonnegativity permits
dropping the weighted energy, and q(1+t)>=1 and q(t)<=q(1+t) give the
positive length witness L(0)+4B. No absolute-curvature cap, product cap,
dyadic construction, endpoint product gain, or RelativeEnergy import is
needed for this direction.

Iff assembly and scope
----------------------
The three final iff theorems compose the five directions correctly:
quarter length iff bounded absolute curvature;
quarter length iff bounded EL;
quarter length iff quarter energy.
All dynamical equivalences retain the actual positive-time normal-FEF
hypothesis. Their scalar components use continuity at zero, so the common
t>=0 domain does not require a backwards extension.

These are exact reductions for a given actual forward flow. An iff does
not prove either side, and the code does not assert a bound witness exists
for every flow. It proves neither global flow existence nor the paper's
unrestricted quarter-power bound, and supplies no counterexample with an
unbounded product. The source's opening scope statement correctly says
the equivalent boundedness properties are not asserted to hold. Distinct
open-problem projects are not imported.
