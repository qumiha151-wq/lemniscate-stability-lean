Their common parameter order matches the approved specification exactly:

For q(t)=sqrt(sqrt(1+t)), d=4c/A^3 and B=E(0)+A^3/(4c), they prove
  E(t)^(-1) >= E(0)^(-1) + d*(q(t)-1),
  E(t) <= B/q(t),
  E(t)L(t) <= A*B.
The scalar hypotheses retain the assumed length envelope L(t)<=A*q(t)
and weak virial D(t)>=c*E(t)^2/L(t)^3. The module does not itself derive
them from a position family. That actual geometric wrapper is a separate
module owned by root. The available actual coefficient c=2^(-21) gives
B=E(0)+2^19*A^3; no sharp 4*pi^2 coefficient is claimed here.

The reciprocal comparison differentiates only at positive interior times
and uses E continuity at zero, so t=0 is included. It does not assume a
backward flow, length derivative, length continuity, monotone length,
continuous D, or an independent product cap. The product cap is obtained
by multiplying the already assumed length envelope and the newly proved
energy envelope; it does not assert that such an envelope always exists.
