The scalar premises are explicit: positive c,A,L,E; E continuous on Ici 0;
E'=-D at positive times; cE^2/L^3<=D; and L<=A*fourthRoot(1+t).
No continuity, derivative or monotonicity of L is needed: its envelope is
used only in a pointwise reciprocal comparison. No energy product cap or
independent decay envelope is assumed.

The derivative of 1/E is D/E^2. Positivity permits the coercivity comparison
D/E^2>=c/L^3>=c/[A^3*fourthRoot(1+t)^3]. Subtracting
(4c/A^3)*fourthRoot(1+t) gives a nondecreasing function. Continuity and
the compact-interval mean-value theorem yield the exact lower bound
  E(t)^(-1)>=E(0)^(-1)+(4c/A^3)*(fourthRoot(1+t)-1).
The fourth root at 1 is 1, and the coefficient 4 is correct. Only positive
interior times are differentiated; the initial and equal-endpoint cases
are valid without a backward extension or derivative at zero.

For B=E(0)+A^3/(4c)>0, both 1/B<=1/E(0) and 1/B<=4c/A^3 hold.
Since q=fourthRoot(1+t)>=1, the exact lower bound is at least q/B.
Inversion gives E(t)<=B/q. Multiplying by the assumed positive length
envelope gives E(t)L(t)<=AB. All inequality directions and nonnegative
multiplication conditions in the source are correct. B is deliberately
nonoptimal; the sum is sufficient without an unjustified maximum argument.

This is a conditional scalar helper. The actual geometric wrapper must
still supply the weak virial bound and all scalar hypotheses from a genuine
forward flow. The source does not itself assert those laws for arbitrary
functions or construct a flow. It neither proves the length envelope nor
unrestricted quarter-power growth. No frozen source was edited and this
