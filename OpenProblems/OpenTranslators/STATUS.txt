Supplementary open-curve translator question: global geometric existence.
13 November 2026. Independent OpenTranslators Lake project.

The actual angle/curvature system theta'=k,k'=v,v'=-k^3/2+sin(theta)
has a unique global solution for every initial triple. Smoothness follows
from the equation. Existence is proved by a bounded globally Lipschitz
regularized field and an actual integral clock; surjectivity of the clock
is derived from energy-radius estimates,not assumed.

Geometry.lean constructs beta(s)=integral(cos(theta),sin(theta)) and the
eternal family X(t,s)=beta(s)+(t,0). It proves joint C-infinity,actual
Euclidean unit speed and immersion,signed curvature and elastic force,
and the geometric normal-velocity equation (X_t dot N)=-F with N unit
and orthogonal to the actual tangent. The pullback metric is exactly u*w
and the actual arclength distance is the standard real-line distance;
the parameter domain is complete.

Initial state(pi/2,1,0) supplies a concrete nonstraight example. Its image
is proved not to lie in any affine line with nonzero normal,and its actual
normal velocity at s=0 is -1 at every time.

This is a geometric translator theorem in the broad complete immersed
class. X_t=(1,0) generally also has a tangential component. A pure-normal
parametrization is not yet formalized. Properness,embeddedness,and global
curvature/derivative bounds are not asserted. The source paper does not
specify these extra conditions in its brief translator question; this
result must not be silently promoted to an unspecified stricter class.
No novelty or publication-priority claim is made.

All source modules import only Mathlib and this project. See
GlobalGeometryFrozenManifest.csv,GlobalExistenceScope.txt,GeometryScope.txt,
and GlobalGeometrySemanticAudit.txt. Other closed-curve open questions
remain separate and are not resolved by this construction.
