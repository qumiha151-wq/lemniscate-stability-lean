Supplementary open-curve translator question; partial formalization.

The Lean proof derives H'=v sin(theta), with H=v^2/2+k^4/8,
directly from the translator ODE, proves sqrt(H+1) is 1-Lipschitz,
and bounds all three state coordinates on any bounded interval.

Local existence, global continuation, complete immersed geometric
reconstruction, properness and embeddedness are not formalized here.
There is no complete-translator existence theorem in this Lake project.

This independent project does not import the closed-centroid, migration,
length-growth or existing lemniscate-stability projects.
