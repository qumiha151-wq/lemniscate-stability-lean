FRENET DERIVATION
k=x'*y''-y'*x'' is computed from actual positions.
Differentiation of actual unit speed gives x'*x''+y'*y''=0.
Algebra using these two identities proves x''=-k*y' and y''=k*x'.

values agree by the complex-argument formulas, so previously proved actual
