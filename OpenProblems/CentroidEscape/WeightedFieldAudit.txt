INDEPENDENT SEMANTIC AUDIT: WeightedField.lean
12 November 2026. Reviewer: length_migration agent.
Scope: concrete equations, weights, tube, clipping and bound constants,
compared directly against validate_certificate.py and arb_shoot.py.
This is a source/math audit; I did not independently re-run Lean compilation
or any of the seven expensive Arb integrations. The author handles compilation.

RESULT
No substantive equation, coordinate, tube, or clipping mismatch was found in
the inspected file. The concrete field-value and Lipschitz bounds are the
ones required by the external certificate, with the intended weighted sup norm.

1. EXACT COORDINATE CHANGE

The Python raw state is z=(x,y,u,w,k,v,m), with weights
 W=(1,1,4,4,16,64,1).
The Lean weighted state a has z_i=W_i a_i, so
 a=(x,y,u/4,w/4,k/16,v/64,m).
Fin 7 -> R has the finite-product supremum norm. Therefore its norm is exactly
max_i |z_i|/W_i, as intended, rather than a Euclidean norm.

The raw Python recurrence in arb_shoot.step is generated by
 z'=(u,w,-kw,ku,v,-k^3/2+xw-yu,x).
Dividing each component by its output weight gives the Lean polynomial field
 a'=(4a2,4a3,-16a4a3,16a4a2,4a5,
       -32a4^3+(a0a3-a1a2)/16,a0).
Every factor and sign agrees. In particular the seventh derivative is x=a0;
it is NOT the x-component multiplied or divided by a curvature weight.
The theorem weightedField_conjugacy records this exact algebraic relation.

2. EXACT TUBE

Python outer bounds for (x,y,u,w,k,v) are
 (8/5,12/5,11/10,11/10,7/2,6).
After division by W they become exactly the Lean radii
 (8/5,12/5,11/40,11/40,7/32,3/32).
No bound is required for m. The final entry zero in tubeRadius is deliberately
UNUSED: weightedTube quantifies only over indices i!=6. It therefore does
not accidentally impose m=0.

The clipping map explicitly leaves coordinate6 unchanged and clamps only the
first six coordinates. The range is weightedTube; it is the identity there
and is nonexpansive in the product sup norm. The lack of a bound on m is safe
because the field has no dependence on m, while m'=x is bounded by 8/5.

3. INDEPENDENT BOUND ARITHMETIC

Using the exact radii above, component absolute-value upper bounds for the
weighted field are, in order,
 11/10, 11/10, 77/80, 77/80, 3/8, 2067/5120, 8/5.
All are less than2. The field-value bound is therefore valid independently
of the unbounded seventh coordinate.

For the weighted Jacobian, exact absolute row-sum upper bounds are
 4, 4, 79/10, 79/10, 4, 1561/320, 1.
The largest is79/10=7.9<8. The code proves the corresponding finite-difference
Lipschitz inequality directly using product and cubic factorizations; it does
not incorrectly assume differentiability of clipping.

These are the same rows checked by validate_certificate.analytic_checks:
 3.5+4*1.1=7.9 for the oscillator rows, and
 (2*1.1+4*(1.6+2.4)+1.5*3.5^2*16)/64=1561/320
for the cubic row. The raw-to-weighted change is consistent in both places.

4. CLIPPED FIELD

clippedField=weightedField composed with clipState is globally8-Lipschitz and
globally bounded by2. Its equality with weightedField on weightedTube follows
from clipState_eq_self. This supplies precisely the hypotheses needed by the
already-proved bounded-field finite-interval existence theorem.

The clipped field is generally NOT differentiable at clipping faces. Thus its
global Lipschitz theorem may be used for existence/comparison, but one must not
silently reuse it as a global C2/C3 theorem for parameter variations. The
original polynomial field can be used for those estimates once the relevant
trajectories and parameter neighborhood are shown to stay in the unclipped
interior. This is a remaining proof obligation, not an error in this module.

5. LINKS THAT STILL MUST BE PROVED WHEN ASSEMBLING THE CERTIFICATE

The conjugacy is an algebraic equality. The later assembly still needs the
corresponding HasDerivAt change-of-coordinates theorem and actual initial data.
The raw shooting initial state (a,0,0,1,b,0,0) is weighted as
 (a,0,0,1/4,b/16,0,0).

The external three-coordinate residual is raw (y,u,v), so extraction from a
weighted endpoint is (a1,4*a2,64*a5), not simply (a1,a2,a5). The norm of this
projection is at most64, as used in the earlier shooting variation analysis.
This rescaling must remain explicit in the future root-map definition.

No Taylor coefficient bound, interval enclosure, first-exit argument,
parameter smoothness estimate, or actual shooting-zero existence is proved
by this field module alone. The independent audit does not elevate these
remaining obligations to verified facts.
