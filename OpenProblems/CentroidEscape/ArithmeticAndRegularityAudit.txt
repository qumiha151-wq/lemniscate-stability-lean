Independent source review, 12 November 2026

RationalBall and BallExpression were reviewed independently of their author.
The seven coordinate expressions agree with WeightedField, including the
1/16 factor in the sixth weighted coordinate and the unscaled moment x.
The product radius is |c|s+|d|r+rs. Contains implies nonnegative radii, which
the proof uses. Flooring the center by a mesh 1/S changes it by at most 1/S;
the new radius includes that amount and is rounded upward. Soundness requires
S>0 explicitly. Division by a natural number has the ordinary Lean semantics,
including zero; actual Taylor recurrence uses n+1>0. No unproved machine
floating-point containment or native_decide is used.

VectorTaylor and VectorPolynomial were source-reviewed for scope. The vector
coefficients are constructed by a recurrence, rather than assumed to obey a
desired bound. The finite polynomial field identity is coefficientwise; the
low-order residual cancels by that recurrence and the high-order residual is
majorized by the previously kernel-checked scalar residual. The clipped-field
version requires a .023 center margin; it does not assert that the actual
global trajectory is inside the tube without further verification.

SmoothReparam computes the actual curve's intrinsic curvature derivatives.
HigherRegularity obtains joint smoothness by bootstrapping the actual time
derivative and the actual exponential Jacobian formula, using parameter
integration on a fixed compact interval. No nonvanishing-field assumption
excludes stationary points. The final theorem retains all exact half-orbit,
reflection-endpoint and positive-moment hypotheses. It does not supply their
numerical verification. Source review found no substantive mathematical gap
in these stated conditional conclusions.

This review does not validate a concrete 750-step interval computation. The
enforced recursive Lean axiom audits, and the precise theorem hypotheses,
remain the authoritative formal boundary.

BallTaylor's executable array recurrence was also checked against its real
coefficient semantics: its constant term includes the initial state, its
square cache is updated before the next cubic coefficient, and the recurrence
is proved against the canonical finite polynomial for n<160. The array
invariant includes sizes and all coefficient enclosures. Horner evaluation
uses 161 coefficients and is proved to enclose the actual polynomial endpoint.
This is an algorithmic soundness theorem, not an evaluation of all concrete
certificate data. A performance run using #eval is not a kernel certificate.

CanonicalStep constructs a bounded clipped-field solution, compares it with
the actual canonical polynomial, and then proves clipping inactive from the
.024 initial-center margin, .023 polynomial excursion, and an error below
.001. Its theorem therefore concludes the original ODE and tube membership
throughout a step of length at most .01. It does not yet concatenate all
750 numerical steps or assert that their concrete margins have been checked.
