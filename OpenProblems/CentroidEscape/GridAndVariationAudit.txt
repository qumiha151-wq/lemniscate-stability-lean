Source review and precise verification boundary, 12 November 2026

BallStep combines the actual polynomial enclosure with the proved local ODE
error. The inflation is polynomialResidual/92, using h<=.01 and exp(8h)<=25/23.
Its executable margin test constrains only the first six coordinates; the
moment coordinate is intentionally unrestricted. The theorem applies to any
actual clipped solution, not merely a newly selected local solution.

BallGrid iterates the actual enclosure algorithm and accumulates all margin
checks. One global clipped trajectory is constructed first. Its grid-node
enclosures and original ODE on every cell follow by induction; the cell-cover
lemma covers joins and final endpoints. BallInterior derives a uniform .0005
interior margin and local equality of the clipped and original fields. Thus
no assumed true-trajectory tube bound is hidden in these conclusions.

FixedBall's shared integer denominator implements the same real interval
meaning. Floor centers and ceiling radii enclose products and divisions;
integer addition/scaling are exact. FixedTaylor and FixedCertificate derive
actual canonical coefficient and endpoint containment from the executable
algorithm or from checked untrusted finite data. Variable-order versions
reduce the number of coefficients and checks, but do not yet supply the
corresponding lower-order differential residual/error budget.

WeightedDerivatives proves actual Frechet derivatives, not formal derivative
symbols: Dg applied to u is weightedLinear; D2g applied to(u,v) is the sum of
the two orders of weightedBilinear. Operator bounds8 and44 and the convex-tube
derivative Lipschitz bound44 follow. This is the original polynomial field;
no false global C2 assertion is made for the coordinate-clipped field.

ShootingVariation constructs the actual finite-horizon flow and uses its
unique solution, initial-value Lipschitz bound, and proved interior margin.
Its small initial neighborhood consists of original-field trajectories.
The actual projection is (state1,4*state2,64*state5), giving physical(y,u,v).
Its quadratic midpoint estimate is derived from the actual field expansion.

MidpointDerivative constructs the limit of actual dyadic difference quotients,
proves its Lipschitz bound and exact midpoint law, and obtains a continuous
real-linear map and an explicit quadratic remainder. ShootingDifferentiability
applies this construction to the actual shootingFlow in its proved initial
neighborhood, yielding HasFDerivAt. This is more than a finite-difference
heuristic. The derivative bounds for the full variable-time three-parameter
shooting map still require their separate interface and estimates.

These proofs do not certify any concrete seven-path 750-step dataset.
The checked finite conditions must still be evaluated with sufficient final
widths, and the actual shooting-map derivative/root/moment conditions must
be discharged. Native execution or an arithmetic timing is not that proof.
