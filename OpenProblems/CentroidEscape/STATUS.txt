Closed-expander centroid escape: computer-assisted existence, partial Lean.

The validated Arb shooting certificate, together with the accompanying
analytic proof, constructs a smooth immersed closed expander with arclength
centroid x > 0.49 relative to its expansion center. Its homothetic flow has
unbounded centroid. Two analytic/code reviews found no substantive gap.
The computation depends on the Python/flint/Arb runtime; the reviews did
not independently rerun all seven high-order integrations.

Lean currently proves:
  - residual + uniform derivative bounds imply an actual unique shooting
    zero by the contraction theorem (zero existence is a conclusion);
  - exact reversal of the six-coordinate polynomial ODE;
  - differentiation and gluing of the reflected actual ODE segment;
  - smoothness on the doubled interval, closure, matching endpoint first
    derivatives, and preservation of the unit tangent;
  - global periodic extension, including actual ODE differentiation at all
    seams, global smoothness and the globally unit tangent.
  - Euclidean Frenet identities, signed curvature and scaled elastic force;
  - the explicit radius R=(1+4t)^(1/4), R'=R^(-3), and the actual normal
    free-elastic-flow equation of the expanding immersed periodic curve;
  - reflection identities for x/y integrals and the moment equation m'=x;
  - the actual arclength-weighted centroid and its scaling, and divergence
    from positive exact half-moment data. The theorem
    exact_half_orbit_yields_centroid_escape assembles these analytic steps.
  - actual unique global parameter trajectories for the bounded periodic
    tangential correction field; logarithmic time makes each moving point
    satisfy the vector FEF equation. Existence is proved, not hypothesized.

Missing from the end-to-end Lean theorem:
  - construction of the exact shooting map/half-orbit and validated
    numerical/ODE estimates discharging the root and moment hypotheses;
  - smooth dependence of the parameter trajectories on their initial value,
    the circle diffeomorphism property, and centroid invariance under that
    reparametrization (the geometric normal flow is already formalized).
Thus the complete centroid-escape theorem is NOT yet Lean-formalized.

This profile does not prove migration: its convex hull contains the
expansion center. No classification or unrestricted length conclusion
is inferred from it. This Lake project imports no other open-problem project.

To rerun the external certificate, install python-flint==0.9.0 in an
environment accessible to Python, then from the certificate directory run:
  python validate_certificate.py --center certificate_center.json --workers 3
The included JSON records source SHA256 hashes, constants and interval
results. Source hashes identify the original successful run. Supplemental
parameter_box_check.txt records the enlarged-time-neighborhood check.
The final ALL ARB CERTIFICATE CHECKS PASSED status is written only after
every check; a failed run is marked NOT YET VERIFIED.
