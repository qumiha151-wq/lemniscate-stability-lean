Finite-certificate soundness and concrete verification boundary
12 November 2026

FivePathCertificate.lean closes the analytic and geometric chain. Its final
theorem takes finite data d and ONE proof hypothesis d.check=true. It then
produces a positive period and a jointly smooth periodic immersed classical
free elastic flow, using the actual intrinsic elastic force, whose actual
arclength centroid tends to positive infinity. It assumes neither an ideal
shooting map/derivative nor an exact root or positive moment at that root.

Finite data consist of a positive integer mesh, three rational parameters,
five paths of751 finite Taylor tables, and a rational3x3 preconditioner.
The paths are the center and the four spatial probes a+/-, b+/-. The true
terminal-time derivative equals the projected field at the center endpoint,
so no L+/- paths are required. Every path still requires750 order60 steps.

The checks include all coefficient recurrences, endpoint gluing, input
margins, rational initial containment, inflated projected endpoint radii,
the time-column field-ball radius, rational matrix row sums and residuals,
the horizon range, and a strict center moment lower bound. The fixed budgets
are delta=1e-25, root radius1e-28, H=6e14, eta=etaTime=1e-40, inverse norm2,
matrix defect1e-9, preconditioned center residual1e-35 and contraction2e-9.

The weighted-state allowance1e-43 is NOT treated as the raw shooting error:
projection multiplies coordinate radii by1,4,64. ShootingProbeBridge proves
the common-horizon transfer, and ShootingTimeColumn proves the actual time
column identity. FivePathRationalData verifies matrix orientation and casts.
MatrixCertificate connects rational Bool row-sum checks to real CLM norms.
The defect bound also proves preconditioner injectivity in dimension3.

ShootingGeometryBridge transports the center moment to the unknown Newton
root with error at most7,000,000*radius, then invokes the proved reflection,
reparametrization, joint smoothness, intrinsic FEF and centroid arguments.
The finite check on the center moment is therefore sufficient; a positive
moment at an unknown root is not hidden among the assumptions.

CandidateEndpointChecks contains ACTUAL finite numerical kernel checks on
the proposed five endpoint balls and proposed rational preconditioner.
Widths, horizon, matrix bound/defect, center residual and moment margin all
pass. Those proposed balls become true ODE enclosures only after the full
concrete grid checks. This module does not assert that missing containment.

FiniteTaylorBlock composes separately proved step/block checks by reusing
their proof terms. Only coefficient0 must agree at a boundary, so the final
table need not carry another unused60-stage recurrence. A finished750-step
segment can then be converted to FiniteShootingPath and its finite check.
This permits incremental proofs instead of one huge Boolean reduction.

CenterFirstTen.checked is an unconditional proof for the literal first10
center steps:600 recurrence stages,10 Horner endpoints,10 input margins and
9 glues. Two five-step blocks are composed without rechecking old stages.
This certifies a partial segment; initial-point containment and the complete
750-step endpoint enclosure are separate requirements of the full path API.

All theorem dependencies are audited against propext, Classical.choice and
Quot.sound. Native numerical generation and external exact Fraction audits
do not supply the final d.check=true proof. All3750 concrete step recurrences
and Horner calculations must still be kernel-checked and assembled before
an unconditional numerical instance of the escape theorem is established.

The constructed candidate's convex hull contains the expansion center.
It is not a migration example, and this work is not a full classification
or a solution of all remaining questions of the paper.
