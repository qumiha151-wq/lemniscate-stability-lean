SIMILARITY PROJECTION AND A PRECISE ROTATING-CONTINUATION OBSTRUCTION
12 November 2026. Analytic deductions; independently reviewed, not Lean formalized.
This note does not classify all closed solitons or exclude all omega!=0.

A. WHAT THE NOETHER IDENTITIES DO AND DO NOT SAY

For any smooth closed unit-speed planar immersion beta, set
 p=beta.N, q=beta.T, F=k_ss+k^3/2, E=(1/2)integral k^2, D=integral F^2.
The exact identities are
 <F,p>_L2=-E, <F,q>_L2=0, <F,Nx>_L2=<F,Ny>_L2=0.
These are the dilation, rotation and translation identities already derived
in similarity_reduction.txt. They hold for arbitrary closed curves.

Let V be the finite-dimensional subspace of L2(ds) spanned by q,Nx,Ny,
and let p_perp=p-Proj_V(p). Then A=||p_perp||_2^2 is STRICTLY positive.
Indeed p_perp=0 would give (beta+bJbeta+c).N=0 for constants b,c;
multiplying by k and integrating gives -L=0. This contradiction does not
require excluding circular curves.

Since F is orthogonal to V, <F,p_perp>=-E. Orthogonal projection onto
span(V,p) therefore decomposes the actual force exactly as

 F=-(E/A)p_perp+R,
 R perpendicular to V and p,
 D=E^2/A+||R||_2^2.

Consequently the scale-invariant nonnegative defect
 Phi=D*A/E^2-1
vanishes IF AND ONLY IF the curve is an exact similarity profile (allowing
rotation and translation). This is an exact equality criterion, not a
replacement of the profile equation by a weaker necessary condition.
The scalar coefficients of -(E/A)p_perp supply its normal similarity
generator. For noncircular curves the generator is unique, by the previously
proved kernel theorem; for circles the normal generator is unique but the
rotation coefficient is a gauge redundancy.

The space V and p_perp are unchanged under a translation of the origin:
p changes by a linear combination of Nx,Ny, and q changes by another such
combination. Thus A, Phi and the normal residual R are translation invariant;
they also have the expected invariance under ambient rotations and parameter
changes. Under scaling by r, A scales by r^3, E by r^-1 and D by r^-5, so Phi
is genuinely scale invariant.

This projection explains why the torque and Gram identities alone have not
forced omega=0. They select the best normal similarity generator and leave
the residual R. Exact self-similarity is the extra equality R=0. There is no
identity above forcing the rotational coefficient of that best fit to vanish.
In particular, claiming that the normal projection of the dilation vector is
orthogonal to the rotation vector merely because beta.Jbeta=0 pointwise
would be an error: the normal-projected cross term is integral p*q, which
need not vanish by algebra.

B. EXACT DYNAMIC DECOMPOSITION OF LENGTH-NORMALIZED ENERGY

Apply the preceding decomposition at each time of an actual free elastic
flow. Put a=E/A>0 and write S=a p_perp, so F=-S+R. The other similarity
components of S are a rotation and a translation. Since integral k*p=-L
and integral k*q=integral k*Nx=integral k*Ny=0,

 E'=-aE-||R||_2^2,
 L'=aL+<k,R>_L2,
 (EL)'=E<k,R>_L2-L||R||_2^2.

Thus every change in EL is caused by the component of the force orthogonal
to all normal similarity motions. A pure similarity motion has R=0 and
constant EL, including a genuinely rotating similarity motion if one exists.
These identities are exact for arbitrary closed flows; no convergence or
boundedness of EL follows without a new estimate on R.

Cauchy--Schwarz yields |<k,R>|<=sqrt(2E)||R||. It alone does not close the
long-time argument. For example it permits positive (EL)' when R is small
and positively correlated with k, in agreement with the explicit m=2/3
initial-data construction. A sign assertion for the last displayed derivative
would therefore contradict known actual curves.

One possible next target is a dynamical bound on the accumulated normalized
shape residual. It must be a NEW estimate: the energy identity only controls
integral ||R||^2 dt in the unnormalized geometry. No such sufficient normalized
bound is proved here.

C. CORRECT DIMENSION COUNT FOR PERIODIC SUPPORT-ORBIT CONTINUATION

For the four-dimensional support ODE
 f_omega(k,v,p,q)=(v,-k^3/2-p-omega*q,-k*q,1+k*p),
fix the phase by q(0)=0, assuming q'(0)=1+k0*p0!=0. Let
 x=(k0,v0,p0,omega,L) in R^5 and define the periodicity map
 P(x)=z(L;x)-z(0;x) in R^4.
The four periodicity equations generically leave a one-dimensional branch.
The separate tangent-angle holonomy is
 H(x)=integral_0^L k(s;x) ds.
A closed planar profile requires H(x)=2pi*m, one further scalar equation.
Therefore five unknowns/five equations is the correct closed-profile problem.
For a fixed omega there are only four unknowns left: imposing both all four
periodicity equations and the holonomy is generally overdetermined. One must
not treat omega as fixed while silently dropping a closing condition.

A robust continuation algorithm uses P=0 plus a pseudo-arclength condition,
not omega as the continuation coordinate: omega can have folds or be locally
constant. One then monitors H-2pi*m along the resulting periodic-orbit branch.
There is also a second important obstruction near reversible omega=0 cycles.

D. LOCAL PERIODIC BRANCHES CAN BE TRAPPED IN omega=0

Let R(k,v,p,q)=(k,-v,p,-q). Direct substitution gives
 f_omega(Rz)=-R f_{-omega}(z).
The periodicity zero set is therefore invariant under
 I(k0,v0,p0,omega,L)=(k0,-v0,p0,-omega,L),
with the radial phase q0=0 unchanged.

At omega=0, start in Fix(R), i.e. v0=q0=0. The half-return equations are
 B(k0,p0,l)=(v(l),q(l))=0.
If DB has rank2, the implicit function theorem gives a smooth one-dimensional
family of such half-orbits. Reflection and time reversal double each one to a
periodic orbit of length L=2l. This family lies entirely in v0=omega=0.

Now suppose also that DP has rank4 at that doubled orbit. The full periodicity
zero set P=0 is then locally a one-dimensional manifold. The doubled reversible
family is a one-dimensional immersed submanifold of it. Its immersion is
nondegenerate because (k0,p0,l) is recovered from (k0,v0,p0,omega,L).
Hence the reversible family is locally an open piece of the full periodicity
zero set. In a sufficiently small neighborhood, ALL periodic support orbits
on this local branch have v0=omega=0.

This is stronger than merely saying the integer holonomy root is isolated:
there is no nearby nonzero-omega periodic branch at all under these rank and
phase hypotheses. Accordingly, attempting to continue this cycle by prescribing
small nonzero omega may fail for a structural reason. To find a chiral branch
through such a reversible family, at least one of the rank/phase hypotheses
must fail (or the branch must be sought away from that family).

This local statement does not classify distant periodic orbits and is not a
proof that omega is globally zero. It also does not claim the rank hypotheses
have been interval-verified for the existing certified profile.

No rank hypothesis has been rigorously verified for the concrete certified
profile here. No global rotation exclusion or migration example follows.
