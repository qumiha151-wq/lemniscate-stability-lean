VERDICT ON THE TWO LEAN MODULES

No mathematical-semantic error or state-order substitution was found.
They prove actual signed local state/Jacobian approximation statements
under only the displayed innerTube, signed-time, and step-size conditions.
They do not prove a concrete long-grid orbit or a shooting root.

1. UniversalStateOrders

stateResidualAtCap N evaluates the residual of the ACTUAL finite scalar
majorant at order N, namely 61 U_N(h)^3-(19/2) U_N'(h). The derivative
sum includes n=0 with zero coefficient; natural subtraction n-1 causes
no spurious constant term. The value/cast lemmas identify this exact
rational expression with scalarResidual N evaluated over the reals.

The order-42 and order-44 residual budgets and the common displacement
bound are separate exact decide +kernel statements. Nonnegative scalar
residual coefficients justify monotonicity from t to h, and abs(t) handles
both signs in the actual vector residual bound. This is not an endpoint
sampling argument or an assumed polynomial remainder.

taylorPolynomial_order_tube uses the SAME N for excursion, residual and
polynomial. The generic polynomial tube lemma first puts the polynomial
in an expanded box, then improves coordinates using derivative bounds.
It does not assume the actual solution tube or infer outerTube directly
from the coarse 0.516 displacement in the narrow curvature coordinate.

universal_signed_trueTaylor_order_error passes that N to the actual signed
segment comparison. It retains the residual norm and 2 epsilon t factor.
The final rounded conclusions are correctly budgeted:
 N=42: residual <= 6e-40, actual state error <= 1e-41;
 N=44: residual <= 6e-42, actual state error <= 1e-43.
Here h=1561/200000, so 2*6*h<0.1. No order-60 residual or state error is
used to bound a lower-order state polynomial.

2. UniversalReducedVariation

mixedJacResidualAtCap M N is the actual mixed scalar residual
 3*(122/19)*U_M(h)^2*V_N(h)-V_N'(h).
The bridge identifies it with jacResidual M N, and N<=M is retained where
coefficient nonnegativity/majorization needs it. The lower-state cases
42/22 and 44/22 are separately kernel-checked to be <=2e-19.

The residual-norm theorem uses mixedVariationResidual a v M N with the
actual state polynomial M and actual canonical variation polynomial N.
The opNorm theorem quantifies over arbitrary v and applies the true
continuous-linear-map bound. No factor six is needed in this analytic
operator argument. That fact does not remove row-sum conversion factors
from separately computed entrywise numerical matrix enclosures.

The genuine fderiv comparison explicitly calls
 signedActualMixedVariationColumn_error ... M N,
and retains forcing 4*EX*(||v||*6/5)+||v||*epsilonV. Its polynomial-tube and
state-error inputs are supplied by the SAME M through the newly proved
generic state theorems. Its residual input is the SAME mixed M,N residual.
For the 42/22 wrapper, EX is 1e-41; for 44/22 it is 1e-43. Neither is
silently replaced by the much smaller order-60 error.

The initial-data derivative is actual: innerTube_flowTubeNeighborhood is
constructed from the clipped flow in a radius-1e-4 ball, including points
on the innerTube boundary. No unproved interior-ball assumption appears.
The signed-time reduction |sigma*s|<=h is used in all four inputs, and
the local comparison includes the sigma factor in the real ODE.

There is one harmless occurrence of 60 worth distinguishing explicitly:

The final directional errors <=||v||*4e-21 for both 42/22 and 44/22 follow

correctly converts the arbitrary-v bound to the actual operator norm of

3. Conditional performance budget

The current script's scalar recurrence matches the formal scalarCoeff
recurrence. Its 42/44/60 rows now use the rounded PROVED residual bounds,
not the tighter unproved decimal values. Other rows remain Fraction
explorations. The emitted booleans compare exact Fractions; printed floats
are display only. The script's header correctly says feasibility, not a
grid certificate or Lean proof.

The nonlinear forcing I*(2*K^2*R^2+rho*R+delta_bound) matches the proved
FlowBootstrap formula. State rounding is accumulated as I*SX; matrix reset
defects enter the multiplier bounded by 1/(1-I*SM). The variational exponent
 I*(4*(K*neighborhood_R)*K+rho)*T+I*SM
matches the actual moving-reference comparison, including the matrix
residual rho. Its rational exp bound exponent/(1-exponent) is legitimate
for these fixed nonnegative constants with exponent<1. Both this denominator
condition and I*SM<1 must remain explicit if the inputs are later changed.

The state-center radius and full parameter-neighborhood radius are distinct.
The script does not replace the full-neighborhood bootstrap by the very
small center radius when computing the variational exponent. The center
physical error K*R, increased to K*R+SX at a terminal post-reset node, only
feeds the center force/center-residual comparison.
The projection factor 16 is consistent with two physical shooting
projections of norm at most 8. The 17*terminal_E term retains the local
field mismatch for the time derivative in the endpoint derivative.

At the reviewed constants the stored report has 42/44 strict center and
neighborhood budgets; the conditional contraction is about 0.0106048 and
self-map/r about 0.0490048. These are conditional arithmetic conclusions.
In particular eta=16*(K*R+SX) is NOT a certified actual shooting error until
the concrete grid, center alignment, and strict bootstrap premises exist.
