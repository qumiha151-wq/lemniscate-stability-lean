EXACT GEOMETRIC DATA
The new ClosedUnitFrenetCurve structure contains actual real periodic
position coordinates x,y, their actual unit tangent tx,ty, actual curvature
and its first two derivatives k,v,w, with w continuous and positive period.
Derivative identities are x'=tx, y'=ty, tx'=-k*ty, ty'=k*tx, k'=v, v'=w.
Unit speed is tx^2+ty^2=1. Position, tangent, k and v are periodic.
No force/stress identity, integral bound, virial relation, translation
identity or final contradiction is a structure field.

The module proves unit speed of the position derivatives, identifies
k=x'*y''-y'*x'', and identifies F=w+k^3/2 with the elastic force obtained
by differentiating that actual position curvature twice. Thus supplied
Frenet fields cannot replace the curve's actual curvature arbitrarily.
An arbitrary-parameter immersion-to-arclength construction is not included.

DERIVED ARGUMENT
The stress coordinates Qx=k^2*tx/2-v*ty, Qy=k^2*ty/2+v*tx are defined.
Their actual derivatives are Qx'=-F*ty, Qy'=F*tx, and both are periodic.
For ANY real translation vector(vx,vy), the translator equation is
 F=vx*ty-vy*tx=-V dot N, N=(-ty,tx).
Projection of stress on V has derivative -F^2. FTC and periodicity give
integral F^2=0. Continuity and the translator-implied periodicity of F
then yield F=0 everywhere. No speed!=0 hypothesis is used.

From the actual positions, p=-x*ty+y*tx and q=x*tx+y*ty are defined.
Their actual derivatives and periodicity are proved. F=0 therefore yields
an actual PeriodicSupportProfile 0 0 period. Existing proved positivity of
the dilation coefficient contradicts a=0. This also directly proves the
stationary exclusion. No independent energy/virial formula is assumed.
