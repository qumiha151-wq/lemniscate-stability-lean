Jacobian approximation errors, a strict contraction and an invariant ball.

length derivative column is correctly the sum of the two endpoint fields.

rounded decimal target or numerical Jacobian is silently substituted.
The generic root interface still requires actual flow/variation bounds
and actual numerical enclosures for the entire parameter cube.

analytic zero interface partly formal, but does not prove the candidate
