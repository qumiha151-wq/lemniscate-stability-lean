INDEPENDENT ROTATING BANACH / EXACT SHOOTING BRIDGE

Owned sources:
  SolitonClassification/Rotating/BanachRoot.lean
  SolitonClassification/Rotating/ShootingRoot.lean
Namespace: SolitonClassification.Rotating.
Imports: Mathlib and this independent SolitonClassification project only.
No other open-problem project is imported. The frozen computer-assisted
certificate files are not modified or treated as Lean axioms.

BanachRoot proves:
- Actual Frechet derivative bounds imply contraction of T(x)=x-B(F(x)) by
  the mean-value theorem on the convex closed ball.
- The actual center residual bound implies that T preserves the ball.
- Completeness plus contraction gives a fixed point. Preconditioner
  injectivity converts it into a true zero of F; zeros within the ball are
  unique. The zero is never an input hypothesis.
- In finite dimension, ||Id-BJ||<1 itself proves B injective.
- Explicit errors in approximate center residual y and Jacobian J propagate
  to actual residual and derivative bounds. In particular the contraction
  estimate is q0+beta*(epsilon+H*r), and the displacement estimate is
  zeta+beta*eta. None of these approximation errors is silently discarded.
- An actual second Frechet derivative bound gives the derivative-variation
  estimate by the mean-value theorem. A strict-derivative interface is also
  supplied for subsequent local C1 flow constructions.

ShootingRoot proves:
- The exact linear initial-data map for parameters (k0,p0,l,alpha,b) into
  normalized state (k,v/8,p,q,b,theta), keeping the two-component phase fixed.
- The actual forward/backward endpoint inputs (+/-l,initial), physical
  output projection Q of norm at most 8, and exact target 112*pi/57.
- Ordinary and strict Frechet chain rules for this exact shooting map from
  actual derivatives of the supplied endpoint flow.
- Differentiating the half-length produces Q(f(endpoint_plus)+f(endpoint_minus))
  when actual time derivatives satisfy the normalized ODE. The plus sign is
  proved from the chain rule, not adopted from a numerical Jacobian.
- A zero is equivalent to matching the four support variables and the exact
  angle increment -112*pi/57. The angle target is not a rounded decimal.
- Explicit actual flow-derivative and approximation bounds imply a unique
  true shooting zero. A further interface derives the necessary derivative
  variation from the actual shooting Hessian bound.

PRECISE REMAINING OBLIGATIONS FOR THE NUMERICAL ROTATING CANDIDATE

These modules are generic analytic implications. They do not prove that the
specific candidate has a shooting zero or a closed rotating profile.
The following concrete premises are still not discharged here:
1. Construct the actual normalized ODE flow on an open neighborhood of every
   forward/backward endpoint for the entire closed five-parameter cube.
   Establish its actual joint C1/strict derivative and the actual variational
   equations, rather than derivatives of numerical polynomials.
2. Prove the local Taylor-series convergence, state and variational tail
   bounds, and all numerical interval containments for the 4000 positive and
   4000 negative segments. Current first-polynomial or arithmetic budgets
   alone do not prove those complete-trajectory statements.
3. Connect every reset and metric tube estimate to the actual flow; accumulate
   state/matrix jumps and prove uniform flow/inverse-flow derivative bounds on
   the full parameter cube, including variable-terminal-time extensions.
4. Derive the actual shooting Hessian bound (intended budget 10^20), the true
   center residual and Jacobian enclosures, and the checked preconditioner
   inequalities from those bounds and exact rational/real data.
5. After a true zero is obtained, apply the independent support-ODE periodic
   extension and integer holonomy/geometric reconstruction theorems to obtain
   the closed nonconstant rotating profile. This is a separate final bridge.

The published Arb/analytic certificate remains a computer-assisted proof at
its documented trust boundary. The present modules do not replace the entire
8000-segment certificate with a Lean kernel proof and do not classify all
periodic solutions or all closed similarity profiles.
