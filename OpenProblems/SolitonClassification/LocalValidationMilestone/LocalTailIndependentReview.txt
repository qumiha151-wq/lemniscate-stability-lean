1. TrueLocalStateTail compares the actually constructed supportFlow with
the finite polynomial at the same exact initial state. Original-field
agreement comes from FlowTube, rather than an assumed solution. The two
paths are confined before applying Lipschitz constant 17. The residual is
p'-f(p), and Gronwall starts with exactly zero initial error. On
0 <= t <= 1/125, exp(17t)-1 <= 34t gives error <= 2*epsilon*t.
No infinite Taylor-series convergence is assumed or needed.

2. TrueLocalVariationTail uses the actual fderiv column and its previously
proved variational ODE. For y=P_actual*v, w the polynomial column and
z=w'-Df(p)w, the implemented identity is exactly
  (y-w)' = Df(x)(y-w) + (Df(x)-Df(p))w - z.
Thus the forcing includes 4*E*C, as well as residual epsilon. Both x and
p are in outerTube when the genuine Df Lipschitz bound 4 is used. The
initial column equals v exactly. The resulting 2*(4*E*C+epsilon)*t is
correct. The polynomial derivative retains the b-direction coupling
and theta component; no state coordinate is silently discarded.

3. PolynomialEnvelope correctly uses degree at most 3*N: the highest
state term is P0^3 and the highest variational term is P0^2*W0. Products
with b/support coordinates are only quadratic. Derivatives have no
higher degree, including N=0. The envelope includes indices 0 through
3*N inclusive and bounds every real |t| <= h, not only an endpoint.
Nonnegativity of coefficient upper bounds follows from their absolute-
coefficient hypotheses; h >= 0 follows from |t| <= h.

4. IntegerEnvelope proves the exact identity
  sum[n=0..M] (A_n/S)*(1/base)^n
    = sum[n=0..M] A_n*base^(M-n) / (S*base^M).
The summation range supplies n <= M, avoiding truncated-natural-exponent
errors. S and base are strictly positive in the denominator bridge.
The fixed-ball numerator is |center|+radius; its rational cast matches
fixedAbsUpper. The integer upper-bound comparison preserves direction.

5. ResidualDataCertificate sets only n < N to zero, justified by the
exact coefficient recurrence. At n=N, the derivative coefficient is
already zero but the nonlinear coefficient generally is not, and it is
explicitly checked. All n=N..3*N are checked. Extended square entries
are checked against the truncated polynomial's full convolution, not
the untruncated formal series. Array out-of-range defaults do not bypass
verification: a missing high entry can pass only if the exact checked
recurrence equals the zero default. TaylorDataValid/FixedTaylorInvariant
provide coefficient sizes and the actual initial containment.
