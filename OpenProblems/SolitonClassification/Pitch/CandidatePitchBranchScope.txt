Geometric interpretation on the continued branch (human argument).
At irrational angular holonomy H/(2*pi), the image closure of the
infinite reconstructed profile is the entire closed annulus r_min<=|x|<=r_max.
Indeed beta(s+nP)=Rot(nH) beta(s) at each point of the support block.
Its rotational orbit is dense on the circle through that point; the
continuous radius on the connected compact block assumes all intermediate
values. No block point leaves the annulus. Its two radii are distinct:
if the radius were constant then q=(|beta|^2/2)'=0 everywhere, forcing
constant curvature. Thus these profiles interpolate between rational
closed members as local analytic ODE curves but their images have annular
closures. This statement concerns this specific support branch; other
solutions at an identical pitch are unrestricted.
