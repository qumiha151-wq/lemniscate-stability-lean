Non-isolation of genuinely rotating closed expander pitches

Theorem. There is an explicit open interval of strictly positive pitches w
in which a countable dense set of distinct w is realized by noncircular
closed free-elastic expanders. The previously certified positive pitch is
a two-sided accumulation point. With w normalized by a=1, use the exact
decimal b0 in the existing shooting certificate. One such interval is

                 (b0-5*10^-34, b0+5*10^-34).

The assertion concerns the global spectrum over all periods and energies.
On this particular branch every fixed EL cap admits only finitely many
closed pitches, even at the shortest possible geometric period. An interval
consisting entirely of closed pitches remains a separate possibility.

Certificate dependency and convention. Use AnalyticCertificate.txt and
shooting_certificate.json in RotatingCertificate/classification_odd_branch_interval.
The proof below requires the human+Arb certified actual ODE tube, derivative
and residual bounds on the radius-r cube, with r=10^-32. Its 4000 forward
and 4000 backward steps were rerun in RootFullReplay. The numerical suffix
check in check_pitch_accumulation.py verifies rational consequences of those
archived bounds. A complete corresponding Lean assembly of the concrete
8000-step hypotheses is a separate task. Differentiability alone at an
approximate trajectory would be insufficient here.

Let c=(k0,p0,ell,alpha,b). In the fixed phase gauge, F(c) has four physical
support recurrences between -ell and +ell, followed by H(c)+112*pi/57,
where H is the actual integrated turning over that block. The rational
inverse preconditioner B is invertible. With e=(0,0,0,0,1), the existing
certificate proves for T(x)=x-BF(x) throughout the closed infinity cube
of center c0 and radius r

         Lip(T) <= q < 2*10^-7,     |T(c0)-c0|_inf < 3*10^-40.
         ||B||_inf < 82,            (Be)_5 = -10.555605451421052.

Each t with |t|<=5*10^-35 defines T_t=T+tBe. It has the same Lipschitz
constant, and its distance from c0 on the cube is at most
3*10^-40+qr+82|t|<r. Banach's theorem produces a unique c(t) on that cube.
Invertibility of B gives F(c(t))=te. Thus each c(t) gives a genuine
periodic support orbit with angle H(t)=-112*pi/57+t. The tube and the
initial bounds v'(0)<-200 and b>0.009 apply to every such c(t).

Subtracting two fixed-point equations for s<t yields

  ||c(t)-c(s)-(t-s)Be||_inf <= q ||c(t)-c(s)||_inf,
  ||c(t)-c(s)||_inf <= ||Be||_inf (t-s)/(1-q).

In particular the last coordinate b(t) is continuous and

    |(b(t)-b(s))/(t-s) - (Be)_5| <= 82q/(1-q).

Every secant slope lies strictly between -10.556 and -10.555. Hence b is
a bi-Lipschitz homeomorphism to its image. The center root satisfies
|b(0)-b0|<=3*10^-40/(1-q), and the endpoints of the t interval are
separated from b(0) by at least 10.555*5*10^-35 in opposite directions.
These two inequalities ensure that the claimed explicit interval lies
strictly inside the pitch image.

The subset of t for which H(t)/(2*pi) is rational is countable and dense.
An angle H/(2*pi)=u/v in lowest terms yields a closed reconstructed
profile after v identical blocks because the support and tangent frame
then both recur. The bi-Lipschitz homeomorphism transports rational
holonomy to a countable dense subset of the pitch image. In particular
H(0)/(2*pi)=-56/57, so the original actual rotating root is a two-sided
accumulation point of realized distinct pitches. The slope bound also
implies plane rotations, parameter phase shifts and spatial dilations
cannot identify different pitches on the branch; reflection reverses
the sign, and all pitches considered here are positive.

For this particular branch, rational holonomy is necessary as well.
The nonconstant continuous support vector has a nonzero smallest positive
period Pmin: its full period subgroup is closed, is not all of R, and
contains the block period. A geometric period of the unit-speed closed
reconstruction is a support period. If the block period and full period
are n Pmin and d Pmin, then periodic curvature gives
Hblock=(n/d) Hfull with Hfull in 2*pi*Z. Hence an irrational-holonomy
member of this branch cannot close after a different full period. This
does not exclude other closed branches at its pitch.

There is an explicit lower bound on the intrinsic energy cost of rational
approximation, without assuming the block itself is primitive. In the old
outer tube, |k|<=8.5, |v|<=36, |p|,|q|<=8.5 and |b|<=.02. Hence
|v''|=|-(3/2)k^2 v-p'-bq'|<=1.5*(8.5)^2*36+(8.5)^2+.02*(1+(8.5)^2)<4000.
Since v'(0)<-200, on 0<s<=1/40 one has v'(s)<-100 and v(s)<v(0).
Thus every primitive support period Pmin is greater than 1/40. If
Hblock/(2*pi)=u/v in lowest terms and a geometric period is L=d Pmin,
the relation d u/v = n m with m the integer geometric turning and n
the block's number of primitive support periods implies v divides d.
Therefore every geometric closing length satisfies L>v/40. For a=1 the
periodic dilation and force identities give E>=L/sqrt(2) and hence

                   EL > v^2/(1600 sqrt(2)).

At a fixed EL cap this bounds v. The bounded block angle then permits
only finitely many numerators u. The strict pitch slope assigns at most
one pitch to each such pair. In particular any sequence of distinct
angles on this branch approaching the certified rational angle must have
denominators tending to infinity and EL tending to infinity.
