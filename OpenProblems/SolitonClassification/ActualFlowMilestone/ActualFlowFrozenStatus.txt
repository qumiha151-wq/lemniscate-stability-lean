WHAT IS PROVED
1. A bounded globally Lipschitz field has an actual unique global flow.
   Applying this to the clipped six-dimensional rotating field gives the
   selected actual supportFlow for every real time and initial state.
   It solves the original polynomial ODE on every actually confined
   trajectory. FlowTube independently proves unconditional short original
   trajectories from innerTube for 0 <= t <= 1/125.
2. Actual ODE midpoint estimates construct the derivative in initial
   state by convergent dyadic quotients. They give its continuity and
   actual joint strict differentiability on confined time/state regions.
   Flow C1 is a conclusion, never a free premise.
3. Differentiating the actual integral equation proves the genuine first
   variational integral equation and its right-derivative ODE. P is the
   actual fderiv of supportFlow, not an independently specified matrix.
4. The actual polynomial field has ||D^2 field|| <= 4 on outerTube, hence
   its actual first derivative is Lipschitz with constant 4 there.
5. Variation of constants is proved for true fundamental/right-inverse
   matrices, including the one-sided/closed-interval interface needed
   by the actual flow. If true P and true Q have bounds K and I, the
   genuine spatial variation of P is <= 4*K^3*I*t*||b-a||.
6. Actual joint flow derivative variation has budget
       4*K^3*I*T + 34*K + 1275.
   For |e0|,|e1| <= 1 the actual five-parameter shooting derivative has
   variation budget
       16*(4*K^3*I*T + 34*K + 1275).
   Arithmetic verifies this is < 10^20 at T=32, K=8000, I=16000.
7. exists_unique_actual_shooting_zero_of_true_matrices derives a genuine
   unique shooting zero in the specified parameter ball from true tube
   and actual P/Q bounds plus the center residual/Jacobian enclosures,
   preconditioner, contraction and self-mapping inequalities. It has no
   assumed flow differentiability, assumed shooting Hessian, or assumed
   shooting derivative-variation estimate.

REMAINING OBLIGATIONS / EXACT SCOPE
The numerical candidate has NOT been instantiated in this theorem.
Uniform tube confinement for the required long time and full parameter
region, true P/Q bounds (including a continuous actual right inverse),
and actual center residual/Jacobian enclosures remain explicit inputs.
The intended K=8000 and I=16000 have not been proved from the 8000 stored
segments by this batch. The interval Taylor tails, metric reset
accumulation, true-matrix/inverse comparison, and full parameter-cube
connection must still supply those inputs. A numerical array cannot be
substituted for actual fderiv P or its actual inverse Q.

This batch proves actual strict differentiability and Lipschitz variation
of its derivative, which are enough for the Banach bridge. It does not
claim a classical second derivative or joint C-infinity flow theorem.
It does not by itself certify the specific rotating closed profile in
Lean or enumerate/classify all periodic profiles.
