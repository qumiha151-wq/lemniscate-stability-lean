2. TWO DIFFERENT STORAGE ROLES

A. Original proof preservation. Each complete original tar.xz includes its
source, all generated proof layers, compiler evidence and endpoint attachment.
Its exact byte/hash identity must have a real published Release asset and an
externally pinned publisher durable record. Keep the original archives and
publication evidence; never replace them with compact exports. A Release is
the intended long-lived store, but do not promise permanent immutability or
assume an immutable flag not actually observed. On retrieval, recheck current
release/tag/asset identity and stream the complete bytes against the pin.

B. Compact staging. A public transport is a reproducible temporary cache:
47 exact primary oleans per job plus the full retained metadata needed to
link those exports to the checked original archive. It is not the original
private-proof archive and does not itself rerun Lean. Actions artifacts may
retain these transports for a stated temporary period such as 30 days. If
they expire, recreate them from the same durable originals/records; never
invent missing receipts or substitute another job's files. Root-owned
publication of compact caches is not required for mathematical provenance.

Preserve the actual Linux48 evidence independently as well. Its existing
artifact is id14007325855 from run43354529340, full ZIP size56200489 and SHA
4664358FBE4B3A93EDD5462626B85BA189BD0E43276A0345FB03CE1893EB1378;
its saved extraction is56062523 bytes. Before ephemeral expiry, retain these
exact bytes in a controlled evidence archive or separately verified durable
store. D509/A0E2 pin the exact23E73 report: recompiling48 and generating a new
report is not a drop-in replacement. Keep source, setup, parsed-header logs,
ordinary logs, all three checked output layers, public_inputs and report.
Their old absolute setup paths are checked as archived provenance by D509,
not followed on the new runner. Do not edit them to localize paths.

Disk planning uses actual manifests at each phase:
- Projection worker: downloaded original compressed archive(s), at most one
 512MiB full-validation scratch, actual compact/output/evidence totals and
 an explicit reserve. Keep original remote assets and local inputs intact.
- Frozen full merger limits: each public<=4MiB, metadata<=8MiB,
 receipt<=1MiB, transport<=16MiB; global public<=1GiB, metadata<=256MiB,
 receipts<=16MiB. It checks actual free space for aggregate totals plus one
16MiB scratch and the configured reserve (default2GiB). These are hard caps,
not predicted actual usage. Metadata aggregate can bind before per-job caps.
- Assembly runner: retain core compressed+restored bytes, Linux48 ZIP/full
 evidence, compact transports, merged primaries/evidence, the compiler's
 second public-input copy, validation/setup/log/index files, new three-layer
 outputs and an explicit free reserve. Count simultaneous copies; public
 only does not mean only one public copy on disk. Measure each incoming
 archive/member total and df before the next stage. Do not stage250 expanded
 original private bundles here. Do not delete originals to fit an estimate.

A new orchestration preflight may impose a conservative output reserve, but
must label it policy rather than measured assembly output size. Stop before
compilation if the actual ledger totals exceed frozen budgets or capacity.
Record real per-phase wall/bytes and, if an external monitor is deployed,
actual peak RAM; never fill missing measurements from a memory-limit flag.
