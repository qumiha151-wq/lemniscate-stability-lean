CIRCULAR SUPPORT EQUILIBRIA: A LOCAL EXCLUSION ONLY
12 November 2026. Independently reviewed analytic proof; not Lean formalized.

For the normalized support system
 k'=v, v'=-k^3/2-p-omega*q, p'=-k*q, q'=1+k*p,
a circular equilibrium is (k,0,-1/k,0), with k^4=2. It exists for every
real omega; rotating a circle does not produce a noncircular rotating profile.
The Jacobian in coordinate order (k,v,p,q) is

 [       0, 1,  0,      0 ]
 [ -3*k^2/2, 0, -1, -omega ]
 [       0, 0,  0,     -k ]
 [    -1/k, 0,  k,      0 ].

Its characteristic polynomial is
 P(lambda)=lambda^4+(5/2)*k^2*lambda^2-(omega/k)*lambda+4.
This follows by determinant expansion; eliminating q from the last two
linearized equations also gives delta_k=(lambda^2+k^2)*delta_p and the
same polynomial. The determinant calculation does not divide by lambda.

For every real nu the real part at lambda=i*nu is
 nu^4-(5/2)*k^2*nu^2+4
   = (nu^2-(5/4)*k^2)^2+7/8 > 0,
where k^4=2 was used. Thus P has no purely imaginary root, for either sign
of k and every finite real omega. Every circular equilibrium is hyperbolic.

Fix omega0. Split the real linearization into its stable and unstable
invariant spaces. Positive definite Lyapunov quadratic forms Qs and Qu can
be chosen with derivatives -|s|^2 and |u|^2 on those respective spaces.
H=Qu-Qs then has derivative |s|^2+|u|^2 for the linearized equation.
The nonlinear error is O(|(s,u)|^3); changing omega near omega0 contributes
O(|omega-omega0|*|(s,u)|^2). Hence H'>=c|(s,u)|^2 in a sufficiently small
common neighborhood, with c>0. A nonconstant periodic orbit wholly contained
there contradicts the strict increase of H over one period.

This excludes a local finite-omega small-amplitude periodic bifurcation from
a circle. It does not exclude large periodic orbits, distant rotating
profiles, homoclinic mechanisms, or a singular limit |omega|->infinity.
It is not the requested complete soliton classification.
