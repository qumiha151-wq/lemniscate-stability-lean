SELF-SIMILAR CLASSIFICATION: PARTIAL RESULTS ONLY

SupportODE.lean proves from actual scalar HasDerivAt ODE hypotheses:
 - the reconstructed planar curve has tangent (cos theta,sin theta);
 - its coordinate Euclidean speed squared is one;
 - its signed curvature is exactly the ODE variable k;
 - its normal and tangent support functions are p and q;
 - its curvature obeys F=-p-omega q, permitting genuine rotation;
 - periodic p,q and an integral-multiple-of-2pi angle increment close it.

The module does not assume the conclusion of any existence theorem. It also
does not prove existence of a periodic ODE orbit, a root for a closing map,
or a classification of all profiles. The analytic similarity_reduction.txt
derives further general similarity and symmetry restrictions; those parts
have received an independent analytic review but are not Lean-formalized.

Namespace: ClosedSimilarity. Independent project: SolitonClassification.
Toolchain and dependency graph match the other separate projects.

OneSignCurvature.lean proves a genuine subclass classification from the
actual periodic support ODE with dilation a>0 and arbitrary rotation b:
 - actual stress derivatives give the force/curvature moment identities;
 - those identities imply signed third-moment bounds by Cauchy--Schwarz;
 - k >= -R/2 everywhere forces k=R, where R=sqrt(integral(k^2)/L);
   the reversed inequality gives k=-R;
 - every nonconstant profile therefore has points k<-R/2 and k>R/2;
 - weak one-sign curvature forces v=0,p=-1/c,q=0,c^4=2a;
 - the reconstructed curve is centered at the similarity center and has
   radius1/abs(c). With EXPLICIT theta'=k and integer angle holonomy it is
   actually closed, has unit speed and satisfies cL=2*pi*n.
Four periodic support variables alone are not treated as planar closure.
CurvatureSignRigidity.txt gives the reviewed human proof. No novelty claim
is made; the sign-changing general classification remains unresolved.

SolitonDilation.lean additionally derives a>0 from the actual periodic support
ODE and L>0, using integral(kp)=-L, integral(Fp)=-B/2 and D=aB/2. The expanding
condition is thus a conclusion in its one-sign and excursion corollaries.
This excludes nonexpanding dilation-rotation profiles in this formulation;
a separate pure-translation generator is not silently included.

FORMAL LOCAL ODD-DIRECTION RIGIDITY
OddLocalRigidity.lean proves a generic local theorem for actual strict
Frechet derivatives. If a zero set is locally reflection invariant and
the derivative restricted to finite-dimensional odd directions is
injective,nearby zeros have zero odd coordinate. The full derivative may
have an even kernel. An accumulating sequence of non-even zeros forces
loss of odd injectivity at its limit. All quantitative bounds are derived
from odd injectivity in the finite-dimensional form.
This is not yet an application to a concrete rotating support shooting
map or a certified numerical rank computation. Full classification remains
unresolved. See OddLocalRigidityScope.txt for exact assumptions and limits.
