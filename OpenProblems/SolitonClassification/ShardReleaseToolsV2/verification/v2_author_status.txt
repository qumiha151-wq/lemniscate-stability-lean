The envelope schema is rotating.release-shards-envelope.v2. Exact helper/source
hashes are required. The original frozen full-archive preflight and canonical
partial 1..32 / full 250 request semantics are reused without editing them.

Draft snapshots derive URLs from the phase-specific release identity. Every
uploaded asset and every draft full redownload are checked against that
snapshot. Explicit 100-entry asset pagination checks the exact complete set.
After all requested assets pass, publication is attempted once; the resulting
published snapshot must have final-tag URLs. Every asset is fully redownloaded
again and the complete set/tag/release identity must remain stable before any
production durable record is emitted. Records use final published URLs.
