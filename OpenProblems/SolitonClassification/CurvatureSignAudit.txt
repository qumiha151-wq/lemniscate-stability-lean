Independent review of the formal subclass theorem, 12 November 2026

The reviewed source is OneSignCurvature.lean, together with the companion
human proof CurvatureSignRigidity.txt. The profile structure contains only
actual k,v,p,q derivative equations and periodicity. The dilation and rotation
stress derivatives, all three moment identities, and the Cauchy--Schwarz
bounds are conclusions. No desired moment estimate is assumed in the input.

The nonnegative cubic is (k+R/2)(k-R)^2, R=sqrt(integral(k^2)/L).
Its expansion has no linear k term, and its integral is C-L*R^3. The signed
moment bound forces this nonnegative integral to vanish. A second continuous
nonnegative integral, R^2-k^2, excludes the negative algebraic root pointwise.
This proves the entire continuous-curve assertion, with strict excursions
in the contrapositive, not a statement about sampled curvature.

The constant-curvature support calculation retains arbitrary rotation b and
proves p=-1/c,q=0,c^4=2a. It fixes the similarity center as well as the radius.
The final closed reconstruction explicitly supplies theta'=k and integer
angle holonomy. It proves the actual derivative speed and period rather than
equating the four-coordinate support period with geometric closure.

No substantive gap was found in the claimed subclass and excursion results.
The theorem does not classify the remaining sign-changing profiles, prove a
migration example, or prove a long-time length estimate. Literature novelty
is not claimed. The general reduction of arbitrary similarity motions to
this support system remains a separately reviewed analytic argument; it is
not silently included in the formal audit.
