# Both complete integer point grids for the winding-one rotating candidate

Both signed arms contain 4,001 grid points. The checked Lean sources include the integer state and all variational entries at each point, together with the approximate inverse matrix and a kernel proof of its inverse and norm bounds. The positive and negative arms share their initial state. At index 4000 each arm agrees exactly, in all integer coordinates, with its prescribed terminal row. [Four boundary row equalities and attachment to closure](../../../SolitonClassification/Rotating/ResetGridClosure.lean)

The grid is split into 63 source files per sign. Blocks 00 through 61 cover 64 points each, and block 62 covers the last 33 points, including the terminal one. The two lookup definitions import all 126 files. Their source and local verification hashes are recorded in the [verification report](GridVerification.json). [Positive grid](../../../SolitonClassification/Rotating/ResetGridPlus.lean) · [Negative grid](../../../SolitonClassification/Rotating/ResetGridMinus.lean)

The remaining finite conditions concern the 8,000 polynomial trajectory segments between these points. For indexed rows passing those checks, the formal geometric theorem produces a smooth closed unit-speed rotating expander with signed turning minus one, sign-changing curvature and length between 60 and 62 in the fixed normalization. The construction and its relation to the unrestricted question are described in the [geometric proof](../Closure/TwoVerifiedTapes.md).
