# A primitive closed rotating and expanding free elastic flow

There exists a smooth, intrinsically unit-speed plane profile with signed tangent turn $-2\pi$. Its shortest positive position period $L$ satisfies $60<L<62$. In the normalization with dilation coefficient one, its rotation coefficient $b$ satisfies $0.10<b<0.11$. The curvature is greater than seven at the arclength origin and strictly negative at another point. The profile is noncircular, and its unique physical generator has both dilation and rotation. [Primitive immersion theorem](../../../SolitonClassification/Rotating/ResetFinalPrimitive.lean) · [Generator uniqueness](../../../SolitonClassification/Rotating/IntegerPhysicalGenerator.lean)

The corresponding physical solution is a smooth immersion for $t>-1/4$, with full coordinate velocity $X_t=-(k_{ss}+k^3/2)N$. Set $r=(1+4t)^{1/4}$. Its image is exactly the initial image rotated through $b\log r$ and dilated by $r$ about the origin. Its length is $rL$. At every future time its convex hull contains the closed disk of radius $r/20$ about the same origin. [Unconditional physical existence theorem](../../../SolitonClassification/Rotating/IntegerFinalPhysicalEvolution.lean) · [Physical identities](PrimitivePhysicalFlow.md)

The closing proof uses 4,000 polynomial intervals and 4,001 grid points for each time direction. Each interval verifies its residual, the bounds on the frame, and both adjacent endpoints. The resulting ordinary differential equation and its first-variation bounds enter the moving-endpoint shooting argument. The reconstructed curve retains arclength, signed curvature, and its actual Frenet position across the seam. The result concerns the specified rotating profile. Exhaustive classification of closed self-similar profiles remains open.
