1. EXACT PARAMETERS, NORMALIZED STATE, AND TARGET

Unknowns are c=(k0,p0,l,alpha,b), with the infinity norm. The phase vector
is the exact decimal/rational pair
 e=(-0.05995393111634394,-0.9982011451324311).
Initial physical support state is(k0,alpha*e0,p0,alpha*e1), with theta0=0.
Use normalized autonomous state x=(k,u,p,q,b,theta), u=v/8, and field

 f(x)=(8u,-k^3/16-p/8-bq/8,-kq,1+kp,0,k).               (1)

The initial-state map z(c)=(k0,alpha*e0/8,p0,alpha*e1,b,0) is linear and
has infinity operator norm at most1. The length coordinate has zero
initial-state derivative. Write Phi(t,z) for the local flow of(1).
The natural shooting map is

 F(c)=Q(Phi(l,z(c))-Phi(-l,z(c)))+(0,0,0,0,112*pi/57),    (2)

where Q extracts(k,8u,p,q,theta), so ||Q||=8. The exact center is recorded
as finite decimal strings in the data. alpha is the EXACT decimal product
of the frozen rho and b center strings, computed with sufficient Decimal
precision to retain the whole finite product. The phase line is kept fixed.
The root cube is ||c-c0||_infinity <= r, r=10^-32.

2. OUTER TUBE AND LOCAL EXISTENCE

In normalized coordinates use the convex tube
 |k|<=8.5, |u|<=4.5, |p|,|q|<=8.5, |b|<=.02;
there is no restriction on theta. Direct coefficient/row bounds give

 ||f||<=75, ||Df||<=17, ||D^2 f||<=4.

Here the nontrivial derivative row for u has absolute row sum
 3*(8.5)^2/16+1/8+.02/8+8.5/8 =14.736875<17.
The p and q rows have sums at most17; the k row has sum8 and theta row1.
For the bilinear Hessian, the u row is bounded by3*8.5/8+1/4=3.4375,
the p/q rows by2, and all other rows vanish. The cubic Taylor coefficient
in the infinity-norm majorization is1/16.

At every center C in the tube, absolute coefficients of f(C+h) are thus
majorized by
 75+17w+2w^2+w^3/16 <= g(w)=75(1+w/10)^3.                (3)
The scalar equation w'=g(w), w(0)=0 has solution
 w(t)=10((1-15t)^(-1/2)-1).
Coefficient induction for the polynomial ODE bounds every normalized
state coefficient by the corresponding coefficient of w; in particular
the n-th coefficient is bounded by10*15^n for n>=1. These convergent
series solve(1) for |t|<1/15. Polynomial local uniqueness identifies the
series with the unique local ODE solution. This proves local existence;
no unproved global ODE existence is hidden in the integration routine.

For an order-N state Taylor polynomial at |h|<1/15, with s=15|h|,
the omitted tail is bounded in infinity norm by

 tau_x=10*s^(N+1)/(1-s).                                (4)

The field coefficients about C are bounded as polynomials for every w>=0;
this coefficient argument does not assume the true path already stays
inside the tube. To obtain the tube property separately, each numerical
grid center is checked against the smaller bounds
 |k|<8.14, |u|<4.125, |p|,|q|<7.31, |b|<.01.
Each step has |h|<.008. On the outer tube, component bounds for
(k',u',p',q') are(36,39.4665625,72.25,73.25).
For all four constrained moving coordinates the inner-to-outer margin
minus .008 times that component bound exceeds .05. A first-exit argument
therefore keeps every local exact reference segment in the tube, with
more than .05 spare margin. The coordinate b is constant. Theta is free.
The same estimates hold backwards in time, using absolute coefficients.

3. LOCAL FUNDAMENTAL MATRIX TAIL

Let X(t) be a local exact state segment from C and let M(t) solve
 M'=Df(X(t))M with prescribed initial matrix M0.
In the coefficient majorization, ||Df(X(t))|| is dominated by g'(w(t)).
The scalar fundamental majorant solves U'=g'(w)U, U(0)=1, hence
 U(t)=(1-15t)^(-3/2).
Its n-th coefficient is(2n+1)*binom(2n,n)*15^n/4^n, at most
(2n+1)*15^n. Thus the omitted matrix Taylor tail after degree N is at most

 ||M0|| * tau_M,
 tau_M=s^(N+1)((2N+3)/(1-s)+2s/(1-s)^2).                (5)

This is an OPERATOR infinity-norm bound, not an entrywise bound. The
matrix Taylor recurrence is exactly the derivative recurrence for(1).
The implementation stores the nonconstant5x5 block: rows(k,u,p,q,theta)
and columns for initial(k,u,p,q,b). The missing b row is(0,0,0,0,1,0)
and the missing initial-theta column is(0,0,0,0,0,1). These are exact
invariants of the full6x6 variational equation, not dropped sensitivities.

4. WHAT THE ARB PROGRAM ACTUALLY ENCLOSES

Every grid state C_j and matrix M_j is an exact dyadic midpoint. Starting
there, Arb computes the finite state and matrix Taylor polynomials and
evaluates them at the exact rational step h=+/-l0/4000. Ball arithmetic
also encloses conversion/evaluation error in the step. The next grid
state/matrix is chosen as the midpoint of that evaluation. The program
records, before resetting, the largest state radius and largest matrix
row-sum of radii. No reset error is silently discarded.

Let X_j(h) and M_j(h) denote the TRUE local state and fundamental segment
from C_j,M_j, whose local existence was just proved. The jumps to the
next dyadic centers satisfy

 d_j=||X_j(h)-C_(j+1)|| <= rounding_x,j+tau_x,
 D_j=||M_j(h)-M_(j+1)|| <= rounding_M,j+||M_j|| tau_M.    (6)

The integrator checks at EVERY grid point, including the endpoint,
 ||M_j||<1000 and ||M_j^-1||<2500.
The inverses are computed with Arb on exact dyadic matrices; a finite
verified inverse encloses the actual inverse. Every reported norm check
is a strict ball inequality. In particular no floating-point SVD is used.
The field Lipschitz bound17 gives on every true local segment
 ||M_j(t)||<=1000 exp(17*.008)<K=2000,
 ||M_j(t)^-1||<=2500 exp(17*.008)<I=4000.                (7)
Thus each local reference matrix is invertible throughout its segment.

N=60 and n=4000 are used for each sign. The checker recomputes(4),(5)
from the exact step and verifies tau_x<2e-56, tau_M<3e-55. It uses the
independently enlarged total budgets
 Sx=sum(rounding_x)+4000*(2e-56),
 SM=sum(rounding_M)+4000*1000*(3e-55).
The finer jump totals emitted by the integrator are also checked below
these budgets. Initial exact-parameter-to-dyadic rounding is included as
d0. The printed balls include the rounding of serialization itself.

5. GLOBAL TRUE-FLOW EXISTENCE AND ERROR WITHOUT exp(17*l)

Concatenate the TRUE local segments just described into a piecewise
reference path X and a piecewise reference matrix M. Both can jump at
the grid points; all jumps are bounded by(6). Compare with the actual
ODE solution x from the exact initial condition, initially known only
on its local existence interval. On any open segment set
 e=x-X, w=M^-1 e.
Because X and M solve their exact local equations, linear terms cancel:
 w'=M^-1(f(X+Mw)-f(X)-Df(X)Mw).
As long as the true path stays in the tube,

 ||w'|| <= C ||w||^2, C=(I*4*K^2)/2.                   (8)

At a grid point,
 w_new=(Id+M_new^-1(M_old_end-M_new))w_old_end
          +M_new^-1(X_old_end-X_new).
The multiplicative jump has error at most I*D_j, and the additive jump
has norm at most I*d_j. Define
 sigma=d0+I*Sx, epsilon=I*SM, T=32.
Discrete Gronwall and(8), stopped at the first crossing of a proposed
radius R, give

 ||w|| <= (sigma+T*C*R^2) exp(epsilon).                 (9)

The checker verifies this expression is STRICTLY smaller than R=1e-46.
It also verifies KR<.001, much smaller than the .05 reference margin.
The first-exit assumption is therefore self-consistent. The tube itself
is NOT bounded in theta. For continuation, additionally use theta'=k,
theta(0)=0, |k|<=8.5 and |t|<32: then |theta(t)|<=272. The other five
state coordinates are bounded by the tube. Thus the actual trajectory
stays in a compact subset of finite-dimensional state space on each
finite time interval (and a slightly larger compact set covers nearby
initial theta values). Polynomial local existence and this compactness
allow continuation past every candidate terminal time; hence the true
ODE exists on the entire positive and negative half intervals.
Its normalized endpoint error is
at most E=K*R. This argument uses the true local Taylor tails; it is not
an arithmetic-roundoff-only calculation.

For EVERY parameter point in the cube of radius r=1e-32, the initial
state displacement has norm at most r. The same argument now uses
R_u=2r and verifies
 (r+sigma+T*C*R_u^2)exp(epsilon)<R_u.
Thus the entire initial-state parameter cube stays in the same tube.
Changing the terminal time from +/-l0 to +/-l adds at most r of travel;
the extra displacement is at most75r. The checker verifies the spare
margin and exp(17r)<2. Therefore(2) is defined on an open neighborhood
of the CLOSED parameter cube, not only at its center.

6. DERIVATIVE AND INVERSE DERIVATIVE ENCLOSURES

Let P be the true variational flow with P(0)=Id along any of these
actual trajectories, and put V=M^-1 P on each segment. It satisfies
 V'=M^-1(Df(x)-Df(X))M V.
For ||w||<=R, the coefficient norm is at most I*4*K^2*R. At a node V
is multiplied by Id+M_new^-1(M_old_end-M_new), as in(9). Therefore

 ||V-Id|| <= exp(T*I*4*K^2*R+epsilon)-1,
 ||P-M|| <= D=K*(exp(T*I*4*K^2*R+epsilon)-1).           (10)

This matrix estimate follows by the integral equation and product bound
for the linear ODE/jump evolution; no numerical variational solution is
assumed to be exact. For inverse bounds, epsilon<1/4 ensures that every
jump inverse exists and has norm at most exp(2 I D_j). Hence
 ||V^-1|| <= exp(T*I*4*K^2*R+2epsilon).

Use R_u=2r in this last inequality. The checker proves the exponential
is below2, both forward and inverse, and allows another factor2 for the
terminal-time extension. Uniformly over the parameter cube and all
needed times, true initial-state derivatives satisfy

 ||D_z Phi||<=4K, ||(D_z Phi)^-1||<=4I.                (11)

At the center, the stored endpoint matrices M+ and M- approximate the
true flow derivatives with the sharper bound D in(10). The approximate
shooting Jacobian uses(M+-M-) times the exact initial-data differential;
its length column is f(C+)+f(C-). The latter plus sign is required when
differentiating Phi(l)-Phi(-l). Taking the physical output projection Q
into account, the full matrix infinity error is at most

 Jerr=16D+16*17*E.                                    (12)

The checker inflates EVERY Jacobian entry by this bound (an intentionally
larger rectangular enclosure). It inflates each center residual by16E,
also conservative. pi is obtained from the rigorous Arb constant ball,
and112*pi/57 is computed by ball arithmetic. Thus the returned residual
balls enclose the exact irrational-angle target, not a decimal surrogate.

7. UNIFORM SHOOTING HESSIAN

The standard differentiated variational equation gives, for initial
state arguments,
 D_z^2 Phi(t)=P(t) integral_0^t P(s)^-1 D^2f(x(s))[P(s),P(s)] ds.
Taking absolute time for the negative branch and applying(11) gives
 ||D_z^2 Phi|| <=32*(4K)*(4I)*4*(4K)^2.
For mixed/time derivatives use
 ||D_z Phi_t||<=17*(4K), ||Phi_tt||<=17*75.
The initial-data map is linear with norm<=1. A combined parameter
bilinear estimate therefore gives the valid shooting Hessian bound

 Hshoot <=16*(32*(4K)*(4I)*4*(4K)^2+2*17*(4K)+17*75)
          <1e20.                                     (13)

All these estimates hold UNIFORMLY in the parameter cube, by Section5.
Consequently ||DF(c)-DF(c0)||<=1e20*r throughout that cube. This is the
required interval Jacobian variation bound; checking the center matrix
alone would not suffice.

8. BANACH ZERO CERTIFICATE

B is an explicitly recorded finite-decimal5x5 matrix, considered exact
rational. Its origin as a floating-point approximate inverse is irrelevant
to correctness: every use of B is rechecked by interval arithmetic.
Let J0 be the rectangular enclosure from(12), and f0 the center residual
balls. The checker verifies B is nonsingular, ||B||<100, and computes

 q=||Id-B*J0||+||B||*(1e20)*r,
 eta=||B*f0||,

requiring q<.01 and eta+q*r<r. Thus c -> c-B F(c) is a contraction of
the closed radius-r cube strictly into itself. Banach's theorem gives a
unique fixed point in that cube; nonsingularity of B makes it an exact
zero of F. The pi enclosure is already included in f0.

The initial physical v' equals8 times the second component of(1).
Its parameter derivative has norm at most8*17=136 on the cube; the
checker conservatively inflates by1000r and proves v'(0)<-200. Hence
curvature is nonconstant. It also proves b>.009 and l>31 throughout the
cube, so rotation is strictly nonzero and the period is positive.

9. GEOMETRIC CONSEQUENCE OF THE EXACT SHOOTING ZERO

The four support variables(k,v,p,q) have identical values at -l and l.
Uniqueness of their autonomous polynomial ODE therefore extends them
smoothly and periodically, with support period L0=2l. The angle increment
over L0 is exactly-112*pi/57 by(2). Put T=(cos theta,sin theta), N=JT,
and beta=pN+qT. Equations(1) imply beta'=T, so this is an immersion of
unit speed. After57 support periods T and N agree because the total
angle is-112*pi; p and q already agree after each support period.
Thus beta is smooth closed with period57 L0 and turning number-56.
Its curvature is nonconstant and it satisfies
 k''+k^3/2=-p-bq with b>.009.

It is consequently a noncircle rotating expanding similarity profile.
The geometric free-elastic-flow evolution uses
 lambda(t)=(1+4t)^(1/4), rotation=(b/4)log(1+4t).
Its normal velocity is(p+bq)/lambda^3, exactly minus the scaled elastic
force. A smooth periodic tangential reparametrization gives pure normal
gauge if that convention is required. This proves existence of ONE
rotating profile after all numerical checks pass; it is not a complete
classification. Its finite rotational symmetry puts the origin in its
convex hull, so no strict-half-plane migration claim follows.
