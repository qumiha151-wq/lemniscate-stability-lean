FORMAL CONTENT
1. The reflected-point difference has norm2*norm(odd coordinate).
2. A strict derivative bounded below on odd directions yields local
   quantitative separation of F(e,o) and F(e,-o).
3. A locally reflection-invariant zero set therefore has no nearby zero
   with nonzero odd coordinate. Both eventual and positive-radius forms
   are proved.
4. On a finite-dimensional odd domain, injectivity of v -> A(0,v)
   supplies the strictly positive quantitative lower bound internally.
5. Hence exists_ball_odd_eq_zero_of_injective requires only injectivity
   on odd directions, not injectivity or surjectivity of the full A.
6. not_injective_of_accumulating_odd_zeros proves the contrapositive:
   a convergent sequence of zeros with nonzero odd coordinates forces
   the limiting odd restriction of the strict derivative to be noninjective.

EXACT SCOPE
F is a map E x O -> Y between real normed spaces. O is finite-dimensional
only for the injectivity/rank forms; E and Y need not be finite-dimensional.
F has an actual strict Frechet derivative A at(e0,0). The zero-set reflection
hypothesis is local and concerns zeros only; F itself is not assumed odd.
No global existence or complete-flow hypothesis is introduced.

This formalizes the generic analytic local-rigidity argument behind the
human shooting-map note. It does NOT yet instantiate F as the actual
rotating support shooting map, prove its finite-time smooth dependence,
prove its zero-set reflection via ODE uniqueness in Lean, or certify any
numerical odd-column rank. It therefore does NOT exclude a concrete
rotating profile or settle the full classification or migration question.
