INTRINSIC SCALAR SIMILARITY PARAMETERS -- 13 November 2026
ScalarParameterUniqueness.lean proves four new statements. Subtracting two
actual scalar equations for the same curvature gives
  (a1-a2) k^2 + (b1-b2) k' = 0.
For a smooth, nonzero periodic curvature and a positive period, integration
over one period proves a1=a2. If the curvature is nonconstant, its derivative
is nonzero somewhere and the same identity also proves b1=b2.

All smoothness, periodicity and nonconstancy hypotheses are explicit.
The positive integral of k^2 is proved from a nonzero point and continuity;
the integral of k' is computed by the fundamental theorem of calculus.
The result applies to the actual scalar equation used in the reconstruction
converse. It asserts neither existence nor classification of its periodic
solutions. A circle is deliberately excluded from rotation uniqueness.
