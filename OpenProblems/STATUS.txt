CentroidEscape resolves the existence question in section 1.2(1). A smooth
closed free elastic flow has arclength centroid tending to infinity.
The complete proof passed all 77 jobs in run 43214648337.

SolitonClassification proves infinitely many pairwise inequivalent smooth
closed homothetic expanders with turning number zero. For all large B at
least floor(c B) distinct shapes have a normalized positive centre
curvature derivative at most B, for a fixed c > 0. Reparametrizations,
similarities and repeated traversals are included in the comparison.
Their numbers of simple inflections in a primitive traversal are unbounded.
See SolitonClassification/SolitonClassification/LinearExpanderCount.lean
and SolitonClassification/SolitonClassification/PrimitiveInflections.lean.
The written crossing theorems bound all self-intersections in terms of
primitive length and show that zero-turn profiles have arbitrarily many
distinct ordinary transverse double points in a single primitive traversal.
Consequently even their plane images have infinitely many topological types.
Lean proves the existence of arbitrarily many ordinary transverse double
points in a least positive position period, including exact preimage
multiplicity. The general geometric upper bound and topological corollary
have written proofs. See SolitonClassification/SolitonClassification/PrimitiveOrdinaryCrossings.lean.
A classification covering every closed self-similar profile remains open.
Andrews and Wheeler also construct infinitely many geometrically distinct
homothetic expanders in their preprint Jellyfish exist.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21227

LengthGrowth gives a sharp lower growth estimate and conditional upper
bounds. The normalized pure expanders show why any general fourth-root
upper coefficient cannot depend on initial length and turning number
alone. Individual fourth-root upper growth for every closed initial
curve remains open. See LengthGrowth/Research/ExpanderLength/.
The integral criterion in LengthGrowth/Research/OneSidedTurning/ controls
length on a single flow by its outward angular speeds at inflections.
If the positive difference between the largest and smallest force derivatives
on the two signed inflection sets is integrable on a tail, the length is
O((1+t)^(1/4)). See LengthGrowth/LengthGrowth/ActualFlow/ExactInflectionGrowth.lean.

Migration gives geometric obstructions to whole-curve migration.
The existence of a flow whose whole image passes from one open half-plane
to the opposite one remains open. See Migration/STATUS.txt.
For closed pure expanders with dilation coefficient lambda and radius R
about their centre, a universal delta > 0 permits lambda R^4 <= 2+delta
while guaranteeing a common centred disk in the convex hulls of any two
positive dilations. See Migration/Migration/UniformHullGap.lean.
Novaga's preprint proves non-migration for initial data satisfying
L times the integral of squared curvature at most 4 pi^2 + 8/3.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07242

OpenTranslators gives an eternal nonstraight complete immersed translating
flow on the line. Its curvature is unbounded. See OpenTranslators/STATUS.txt.

CentroidEscape answers the existence question affirmatively. There is a
smooth closed immersed normal free elastic flow whose arclength centroid's
x-coordinate tends to positive infinity. The complete numerical and analytic
certificate, including the final existence theorem, passed Lean in run
43214648337. See CentroidEscape/FullVerification.txt.

LengthGrowth proves the universal sharp lower coefficient
limsup L(t)^4/t >= 32*pi^4. The once-covered expanding circle attains
equality. The fourth-root upper bound for unrestricted data remains open.
For a given flow let G=sqrt(L)*|int k^3 ds|/(int k^2 ds)^(3/2).
There is an explicit universal g_*<1/2 such that eventual G>g_* gives
both quarter-power bounds. Every turning-zero flow has liminf G<=g_*.
The finite-window mathematical proof specifies the constant in
LengthGrowth/Research/CurvatureMomentGap/FiniteWindowThreshold.md.
The complete finite-window proof now passes Lean, including the static
estimate for arbitrary regular periodic positions. The final flow theorem
requires only the geometric flow hypotheses and its stated moment
condition. A separate theorem covers all flows of turning number zero.
See LengthGrowth/Research/CurvatureMomentGap/ExplicitThresholdVerification.md.
For each fixed nonzero turning number there is also a gap below one
in liminf of the centered moment J outside the round circle basin.
Over all flows liminf J<=1, with equality precisely for smooth
convergence to the appropriate circle under length normalization.
See CurvatureMomentGap/CriticalMomentRigidity.md in LengthGrowth/Research
for compactness, finite-window rigidity, and the circle stability input.
If G>=1 eventually, the normalized curvature oscillation tends to zero.
The proof, quantitative bounds and the distinction between checked Lean
statements and applications of the circle-stability theorems are in
LengthGrowth/Research/CurvatureMomentFormalization.txt and
LengthGrowth/Research/LengthCurvatureSkewness.md. This includes every
flow whose signed curvature eventually has one sign on each time slice.
At fixed turning number one the Andrews--Wheeler jellyfish have unbounded
asymptotic coefficients as their symmetry order increases, while every
individual curve in that family has exact fourth-root growth.

Migration proves a necessary energy cost for the whole curve to move
between opposite open half-planes in time T. In particular,
T E(0)^3 (E(0)-E(T)) >= 16/(pi+2)^2. For a pure homothetic expander
of rate a and centered radius R with a R^4 <= 2, its expansion center
lies in the interior of its convex hull, excluding migration for this
class. Novaga's preprint arXiv:2611.07242 excludes migration when the
initial L times the integral of squared curvature is at most 4*pi^2+8/3.
The verified dense-pitch family has a fixed arclength centroid and
cannot migrate across a line. Its cyclic geometry and the corresponding
Migration lemmas are described in Migration/Research/RotatingProfiles.md.
The unrestricted question remains open.

SolitonClassification now provides a closed noncircular free elastic
flow with genuine rotation and a dense set of positive pitches of closed
profiles in a nonempty interval. The no-hypothesis Linux results are in
runs 44650976356 and 44657378217. VerifiedRotatingProfiles/README.md
links to the source packages, audits and a permanent GitHub release.
For all periodic support profiles the centered cubic ratio satisfies
|2h-s|<=1, with equality precisely on centered circles. The inequality
and equality characterization are kernel-checked. SolitonClassification/
Research/CubicGap.md also gives a positive gap at any fixed turning
without an energy bound, using the mathematical static estimate.
The complete classification remains open. The local classification near
the particular rotating orbit combines rigorous enclosures with a
hyperbolicity argument; see RotatingCertificate/LocalClassification
in that project for its mathematical and formal verification scopes.
Andrews and Wheeler's preprint arXiv:2601.21227 constructs infinitely
many purely homothetic expanders.

OpenTranslators treats an additional question about complete open curves.
Its nonstraight translator has unbounded curvature.
