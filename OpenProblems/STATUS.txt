Status at 12 November 2026

These are independent partial formalizations, not solutions of all the
remaining questions in Miura--Wheeler arXiv:2404.12619v3.

Existing lemniscate stability: the original repository root project.
LengthGrowth/: scalar dissipation-to-secant theorem and conditional
  fourth-power bound from a uniform normalized-energy bound. PDE identities and
  unrestricted quarter-power growth remain outside the formalization.
  The reviewed FixedTurningObstruction.txt gives actual closed curves of every
  fixed turning number with arbitrarily high EL and initially increasing EL,
  as well as D/E^5 tending to zero. This is a human proof of an obstruction
  to two proposed methods; it is not a long-time counterexample or a Lean proof.
  CoercivitySharpness.txt further rules out every homogeneous static coercivity
  bound with energy exponent greater than 2, even at fixed turning number.
  Its examples have initially decreasing EL, and do not settle long-time growth.
Migration/: scalar one-sided homothetic migration criterion. No existence
  theorem for a one-sided closed free-elastic-flow expander is supplied.
SolitonClassification/: independent support-function ODE and actual geometric
  reconstruction. The reviewed analytic note reduces all noncircular similarity
  motions to fixed-center spiral expanders, but does not classify their profiles.
  The reduction note as a whole is not Lean-formalized.
  New formal subclass: actual periodic similarity profiles of one-sign
  curvature are centered circles. Nonconstant profiles must have positive
  and negative curvature excursions exceeding half their RMS curvature.
  Closed reconstruction includes the explicit integer angle condition.
CentroidEscape/: computer-assisted existence proof of a closed expander
  with nonzero arclength centroid, hence a flow with unbounded centroid.
  Seven rigorous Arb integrations passed; two analytic/code reviews found
  no substantive gap. The complete theorem is not yet Lean-formalized:
  Lean proves the residual/derivative-to-root implication and the reflected
  ODE's global periodicity/smoothness/unit-tangent bridge, Euclidean geometry,
  actual normal flow, and the arclength-centroid integral/divergence bridge.
  Generic bounded-ODE existence and validated-integration lemmas now establish
  error propagation and the numerical error budget; their concrete shooting
  inputs remain to be verified.
  Concrete weighted field and clipping bounds, scalar finite-polynomial
  residual bounds, actual parameter-flow inverses, C1 lift/inverse and exact
  arclength-centroid invariance are also now formalized. Actual vector Taylor
  construction and uniform residual bounds, outward rational-ball arithmetic,
  the intrinsic force of the reparametrized curve, and joint C-infinity
  regularity are now proved. The concrete interval shooting certificate is
  still incomplete. See CentroidEscape/STATUS.txt for the numerical gaps.
  A checked-grid-to-actual-ODE theorem, integer certificate checker, variable
  polynomial order, actual field derivatives and initial-value/midpoint
  estimates are now formalized. The concrete seven-path certificate is not.
OpenTranslators/: supplementary complete-open-curve translator estimates
  derived from the actual ODE. Global continuation and geometric existence
  are not yet formalized.

Each populated directory is a separate Lake project with its own root
module and enforced axiom audit. Build it from that directory. No proof
module imports a different open-problem project.

Literature checked:
Andrews--Wheeler, Jellyfish exist, arXiv:2601.21227: infinitely many new
dihedral expanders, not a full classification. Numerical asymmetric profiles
are described as "one-sided", with rigorous existence left open. That wording
does not assert strict half-plane separation from the expansion center and
does not supply a migration example. See Migration/SourceTerminologyAudit.txt.
Novaga, Non-migration for planar elastic flows, arXiv:2611.07242 (preprint,
4 November 2026): non-migration under L integral k^2 <= 4 pi^2 + 8/3,
not for unrestricted initial data.

The accompanying Progress.tex contains the earlier human-readable partial
estimates; it predates the new centroid certificate. Current per-project
STATUS.txt files are authoritative for the additional work. Differential-
geometric arguments in those notes are not claimed to be Lean-verified.
