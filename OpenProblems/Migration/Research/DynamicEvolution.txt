ACTUAL EVOLUTION OF THE BEST-DILATION DECOMPOSITION
13 November 2026. Analytic research; the time-evolution results below
are not Lean formalized. General Migration and LengthGrowth remain open.

The main new dynamic result here is the evolution equation for the
regression center, not a scalar budget imposed on a curve. It yields a
conditional, scale-invariant nonmigration criterion. A certified actual
initial-curve test also disproves the candidate Lyapunov functional E^3 J.

1. ACTUAL FLOW AND NOTATION

Use a fixed material parameter u on the closed curve, g=|gamma_u|,
partial_s=g^(-1)partial_u, N=JT, and the actual normal flow

  gamma_t=-F N,  F=k_ss+k^3/2,  g_t=kF g,
  T_t=-F_s N,  N_t=F_s T.

All time derivatives in this note are at fixed material u before
differentiating the arclength integrals. Define, at each time,

  E=(1/2)integral k^2 ds, D=integral F^2 ds,
  A=integral N tensor N ds,
  d=A^(-1)integral (gamma dot N)N ds,
  p=(gamma-d) dot N, q=(gamma-d) dot T, J=integral p^2 ds,
  a=E/J, w=F+a p, Delta=integral w^2 ds=D-E^2/J.

The independent Migration Lean modules now prove from actual smooth
closed unit-speed coordinates that A is positive definite, d is the
unique normal-distance least-squares center, E,J,a>0, and

  integral pN=0, integral FN=0, integral Fp=-E,
  integral wp=0, integral wN=0, integral kp=-L.

Along a smooth flow A and the moment defining d vary smoothly, so d is
smooth in time. Positivity of A makes differentiating its defining
balance legitimate. The spatial identities are

  p_s=-kq,  q_s=1+kp.                                  (1)

2. PROJECTED LENGTH AND ENERGY EVOLUTION

Put B=integral k w ds. The actual PDE gives exactly

  L'=aL+B,  E'=-aE-Delta,
  (EL)'=E B-L Delta.                                   (2)

Orthogonality to p improves the Cauchy--Schwarz coefficient. Set

  k_perp=k+(L/J)p,
  K=integral k_perp^2 ds=2E-L^2/J >=0.

Then B=integral k_perp*w and

  B^2<=K Delta.                                        (3)

This is an actual projected estimate, not an assumed dissipation law.
It still does not imply monotonicity of EL: the term E B can be positive.

For transparent scale-free variables, write

  U=EL, eta=2EJ/L^2-1>=0, delta=Delta*J/E^2>=0,
  d tau=a dt, chi=B/(aL).

Then

  |chi|<=sqrt(eta*delta),
  d(log E)/d tau=-1-delta,
  d(log L)/d tau=1+chi,
  d(log U)/d tau=chi-delta<=sqrt(eta*delta)-delta<=eta/4. (4)

In particular actual increase of U requires

  0<delta<eta, equivalently 0<Delta<E^2*K/L^2,           (5)

as well as positive correlation B. When K=0, U'=-L Delta<=0.
Large residual dissipation decreases U; possible increase lies in the
small-residual cone (5). No proof that a trajectory cannot spend enough
time in that cone has been obtained.

For example, finite integral eta d tau would imply bounded U by (4),
and hence the previously proved quarter-growth implication. But this
condition is not known and is stronger than necessary: on a noncircular
homothetic expander eta can be a nonzero constant, tau diverges, and U
is nevertheless constant because delta=0. This is a criterion, not a
resolution of the unrestricted length problem.

3. A GENUINE NEW CENTER-VELOCITY IDENTITY

Differentiate integral pN ds=0. The actual material support derivative is

  p_t=-F-d' dot N+qF_s.

Therefore

  A d'=integral [(-F+kFp)N+F_s(qN+pT)] ds.

Using (1),
  (qN+pT)_s=(1+2kp)N-2kqT.
Integration by parts and integral FN=0 yield

  A d'=integral kF(2qT-pN) ds.                         (6)

Define the actual vector field H=k(2qT-pN). A second spatial identity,

  (p^2 T)_s=-pH,

shows integral pH=0. Substitution of F=-ap+w in (6) now cancels the
entire pure-dilation contribution:

                   A d'=integral wH ds.              (7)

Thus regression-center motion is driven ONLY by the residual w. This
differs from centroid motion, which has the additional a(c-d) term.
In particular Delta=0 at a time forces d'=0 at that time. No assumption
that d is fixed or that it belongs to the convex hull was made in deriving
(7). This is an actual position-evolution statement beyond the static
force decomposition and beyond the previous scalar energy budgets.

4. AN OPTIMIZED DIRECTIONAL MOBILITY COEFFICIENT

For a fixed unit vector e define

  h_e=(A^(-1)e) dot H,
  z_e=A^(-1)integral h_e N ds,
  htilde_e=h_e-z_e dot N,
  C_e^2=integral htilde_e^2 ds.

This subtracts the L2 projection onto the two normal components. Since
integral pH=0 and integral pN=0, htilde_e is also orthogonal to p.
Equation (7) and integral wN=0 give the exact identity and bound

  d' dot e=integral w htilde_e ds,
  |d' dot e|<=C_e sqrt(Delta).                          (8)

This is the optimal Cauchy--Schwarz coefficient using the residual norm
and the proved normal/support orthogonality. Let C=sup_{|e|=1} C_e if a
direction-independent estimate is desired. It is finite: A is positive
definite and all integrands are smooth on the compact curve.

Take the actual energy radius R=pi/E. Its evolution is

  R'=pi D/E^2=(pi/J)(1+delta)>0.

Consequently

  |d' dot e|/R'
    <= (E sqrt(J) C_e/pi)*sqrt(delta)/(1+delta)
    <= beta_e,  beta_e:=E sqrt(J) C_e/(2*pi).           (9)

The quantity beta_e is invariant under ambient dilations: C_e scales
as length^(-1/2). The force-sensitive first bound is sharper than its
last, shape-only bound; its scalar factor is largest at delta=1 and
tends to zero both as delta->0 and delta->infinity.

This replaces the length centroid by a center whose mobility vanishes
under exact homothety. It does not assert a universal bound on beta_e.

5. CONDITIONAL ACTUAL NONMIGRATION, WITHOUT AN ENERGY THRESHOLD

Define margins relative to d, not the centroid:
  r_e^+=max gamma dot e-d dot e,
  r_e^-=d dot e-min gamma dot e.
They need not be positive for a general curve.

Proposition. Suppose along a time interval and for one fixed direction e
there is a constant q0>0 such that

  r_e^+(t)>=q0 R(t), r_e^-(t)>=q0 R(t), beta_e(t)<=q0   (10)

throughout that interval. Then the actual curve cannot migrate entirely
from the upper half-plane to the lower half-plane in direction e between
any two times of that interval. The same conclusion holds if beta_e in
(10) is replaced by the sharper first coefficient in (9).

Proof. A crossing at t0<t1 would imply
  (d(t0)-d(t1)) dot e >= r_e^-(t0)+r_e^+(t1)
                       >=q0(R(t0)+R(t1)).
But (8)--(10) and E'=-D imply
  |(d(t1)-d(t0)) dot e|<=q0(R(t1)-R(t0)),
which contradicts R(t0)>0. No embeddedness, turning number, or bound on
EL occurs in this proof. For a single specified pair of times it suffices
to assume the margin bounds at those endpoints and the mobility bound
between them.

Round circles, including multiple covers, have htilde_e=0, so beta_e=0.
This confirms that the criterion is not restricted to the once-covered
small-energy sublevel. It is nevertheless a CONDITION ALONG THE ACTUAL
FLOW. No initial-data condition ensuring propagation of (10) has been
proved. Center-in-hull and positive normalized margins cannot be silently
assumed; both are genuine restrictions on the shapes.

An additional conditional consequence of (9): if beta is uniformly
bounded and E has a positive limit, then d has finite total variation
and converges, because R has a finite limit. This alone gives neither
nonmigration nor a contradiction to length divergence.

6. THE NORMAL-DISTANCE MOMENT ALSO HAS AN EXACT EVOLUTION

The balance removes the d' term when differentiating J:

  J'=integral [2p(-F+qF_s)+kFp^2] ds
    =-4 integral pF ds+integral kF(2q^2-p^2) ds
    =4E+integral kF(2q^2-p^2) ds.                     (11)

The identity
  (q p^2)_s=p^2-kp(2q^2-p^2)
implies integral kp(2q^2-p^2)=J. Hence with
  C_J=integral kw(2q^2-p^2) ds,
one obtains

  J'=3E+C_J,
  (E^3 J)'=E^3 C_J-3E^2 J Delta.                     (12)

The natural scale-invariant candidate E^3J therefore has an uncontrolled
linear residual term, not just a negative square. This is not merely a
failure of the present estimate: Section 8 gives a certified actual
initial curve where its derivative is strictly positive.

For completeness,
  d(log(1+eta))/dt=C_J/J-2B/L-Delta/E.                 (13)
No favorable sign for the new geometric term C_J was found. Replacing
the unclosed quantity U by eta or E^3J does not close the dynamics.

7. A HISTORY-DEPENDENT MONOTONE NORMALIZATION

Let lambda(t)=exp(integral_0^t a(r)dr), lambda(0)=1. Equations (2) give

  (lambda E)'=-lambda Delta<=0,
  lambda(t)E(t)/E(0)
    =1-(1/E(0))integral_0^t lambda(r)Delta(r)dr.        (14)

The ratio in (14) is scale invariant and genuinely nonincreasing, and
the weighted residual action is finite. However, lambda depends on the
entire history, not just the current unparameterized shape. In particular
if E_infinity>0, (14) only makes lambda bounded; it does not contradict
any established long-time estimate. Calling (14) an unconditional static
shape Lyapunov functional, or using it to assume E_infinity=0, would be
incorrect. The residual effect on L/lambda and on J/lambda^3 remains
uncontrolled through B and C_J.

8. CERTIFIED FAILURE OF E^3 J AS A LYAPUNOV FUNCTIONAL

Use the explicit smooth closed curve, with exact rational b=99/100,

  gamma_b(u)=( cos(u)/(1+sin(u)^2),
               b sin(u)cos(u)/(1+sin(u)^2) ), 0<=u<=2*pi.

Reflection in each coordinate axis preserves its image with arclength
measure. Uniqueness and equivariance of the regression center force d=0.
Write r=sin(u)^2, H0=1+r and

  P=r(3-r)^2+b^2(1-3r)^2
   =r^3+(9b^2-6)r^2+(9-6b^2)r+b^2.

The actual speed is g=sqrt(P)/H0^2. Since 0<b<=1,
  P>=b^2(1+r)^3, hence g^2>=b^2/2>0.
Thus this is an actual globally smooth regular closed initial curve.
No scalar data are being passed off as geometric realizability.

The following formulas derive the actual intrinsic force used in the
certificate; primes on f,P below mean differentiation with respect to r:

  f=3b H0^4/P^(3/2), k=cos(u) f,
  ell=f'/f=4/H0-(3/2)P'/P,
  ell'=-4/H0^2-(3/2)(P''/P-(P'/P)^2),
