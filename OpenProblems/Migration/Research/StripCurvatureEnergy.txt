Uniform bending energy in a strip and the time cost of migration
20 November 2026

Fix a smooth regular closed immersed plane curve gamma, and choose coordinates
so that the strip is horizontal. Write (X,Y) for the unit tangent, k for signed
curvature, ds for arclength, W for the vertical span, and E = (1/2) int k^2 ds.

Rolle's theorem for the vertical position supplies a point j with Y(j)=0.
Rolle's theorem for horizontal position on the full period starting at j
supplies an interior p with X(p)=0. Thus Y(p) is either 1 or -1. Let sigma
denote that sign. Take the last zero a of Y before p and the first zero b
after p in this period. Both exist by compactness. By continuity, sigma Y is
nonnegative on [a,b]. At a and b the horizontal tangent component has
absolute value 1. Hence the total variation of X on each of [a,p] and [p,b]
is at least 1. The Frenet identity dX/ds=-kY gives

  2 <= int_a^b |k| |Y| ds.

Cauchy-Schwarz bounds the square of the last integral by

  (int_a^b k^2 ds) (int_a^b Y^2 ds)
    <= (2E) int_a^b sigma Y ds
    = (2E) sigma (height(b)-height(a))
    <= 2E W.

The full bending integral over the shifted material period equals the one
over the defining period by periodicity. Thus E W >= 2, for every turning
number and every smooth regular material parametrization.

Now consider an actual normal free elastic flow over [0,T] whose entire
initial image lies above a line and whose entire final image lies below it.
Let E0 and ET be the endpoint energies, W0 and WT the vertical spans. Write

  C(W,E) = W^3/(pi+sqrt(2EW))^2.

The previously verified two-endpoint bound reads

  C(W0,E0) + C(WT,E0) <= T(E0-ET).

The static bound gives E0 W0 >= 2 and ET WT >= 2. The energy is
nonincreasing, so E0 WT >= 2 as well. For x=E0 W >= 2 one has
sqrt(2x) <= x, and

  2/(pi+2) <= x/(pi+sqrt(2x)).

Squaring and multiplying by x yields E0^3 C(W,E0) >= 8/(pi+2)^2.
Adding this inequality at both endpoints gives

  T E0^3 (E0-ET) >= 16/(pi+2)^2.

The forward-time version requires smooth regular normal free elastic flow
only at positive times, plus joint continuity of position and right
continuity of bending energy at initial time. Continuity of the extrema
of a continuous family over the compact parameter interval gives continuity
of W. Passing to the right limit proves E0 W0 >= 2 under these assumptions.
The lower bound concerns the cost of a crossing. Whether a crossing can
ever be realized at unrestricted times remains open.
