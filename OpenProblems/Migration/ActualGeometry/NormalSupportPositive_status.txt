coordinates x,y, positive L, full L-periodicity of both coordinates,
actual unit speed on [0,L], and an arbitrary point d:Fin 2->R. It proves

Proof: suppose the normal support vanishes throughout the period. Actual
periodicity makes a maximum of squared distance on [0,L] a global maximum.

there. The unit-speed coordinate identity then forces the maximal squared
distance to be zero. Both coordinates would be constant everywhere,
contradicting actual unit speed. Thus support is nonzero somewhere;
continuity gives a strictly positive squared integral.
