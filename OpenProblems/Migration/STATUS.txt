now have Lean proofs. Write W(t) for the vertical span of the image and E(t)

strict separation across a common horizontal line, set

  C(W,E) = W^3/(pi+sqrt(2*E*W))^2.

Theorems whole_halfplane_energy_cost and forward_halfplane_energy_cost give

  C(W(0),E(0)) + C(W(T),E(0)) <= T*(E(0)-E(T)).

The first theorem assumes smooth immersed normal free elastic flow on an
open time set containing the slab. The forward theorem assumes smooth
immersed normal flow at positive times, joint position continuity up to
initial time and right continuity of bending energy there. The constants
come from actual endpoint images and the initial energy. Existence or
exclusion of migration at unrestricted times remains open.
